The preview is over. This is the game that launched on October 6, 2026.

You race as Shade in a runs-based tour. Between events, Hibi sells parts. On track you shunt, slam, and take rivals down. Four classes exist. Three of them share a grid. Pods race alone because they are faster and larger.

Two boosts sit on every serious build. Afterburner is the one you can use whenever the meter is up. Ramjet is stronger, has to be held, and cooks the speeder if you stay on it. Kinetic Burst is a bike ability, not a third boost you can bolt onto a landspeeder. Knife Edge is the skim speeder's lean. Neither is optional if you picked that class.

The road does not mark itself. First-weekend players describe boosting into a wall the line did not announce. Learn the planet once. Then use the ramjet.
