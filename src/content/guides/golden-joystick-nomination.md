Steam's news feed lists **STAR WARS: Galactic Racer** as nominated for a Golden Joystick Award. A second post is a photo-mode competition. Neither post is a patch note, a review score, or a price change.

The live numbers next to that nomination, checked October 16, 2026: Very Positive, 4,983 positive out of 5,767 English reviews, **$59.99**, 49 achievements, Windows on Steam, launched October 6, 2026.

## What to do with the nomination

Treat it as attention, not as a settings fix. HDR black screens, lobby size, rubberbanding, and mid-race stutters still need the support pages:

- [HDR and black screens](/hdr-black-screens/)
- [Rubberbanding AI](/rubberbanding-ai/)
- [DLSS 4.5 settings](/tips/dlss-45-settings/)

A photo-mode contest is a capture event. It does not add a new track or a private lobby.
