Checked October 16, 2026: English Steam reviews for **STAR WARS: Galactic Racer** are **Very Positive**, 4,983 positive out of 5,767. Steam App 4078430 lists the game at **$59.99** with no discount. Forty-nine achievements. The storefront date is October 6, 2026.

Very Positive ten days after launch is a racing score, not a clean bill for every lobby, HDR mode, or mid-race hitch.

## What the count does not settle

- Lobbies and private-session limits stay on [no private lobbies](/tips/no-private-lobbies/).
- HDR and black screens stay on [HDR and black screens](/hdr-black-screens/).
- A hitch every few races is a different bug from a failed boot check. See [race stutters](/tips/race-stutters/).
