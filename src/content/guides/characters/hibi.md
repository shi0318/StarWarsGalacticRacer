Hibi is not a racer you pick. She is the reason the garage works. Fuse Games describes her as Shade's mechanic and the loudest person in the paddock: an Ardennian who fits parts, sells upgrades, and will tell you when an experimental piece is about to cook the speeder.

Her voice is a Liverpool accent. That was a casting choice, not a random extra. It is the easiest way to pick her out of a crowded paddock cutscene.

## What the workshop actually does

Every planet's paddock has her shop. Between events you fit parts you earned in the last race or buy them from her. Those parts are stat boosts and abilities, not cosmetics. Livery is a separate editor. Do not spend the race budget painting the hull if the ramjet is still stock.

The loop is short on purpose:

1. Finish the event.
2. Walk the paddock. Rivals talk, challenge, and occasionally hand you a part lead.
3. Open Hibi's bench and fit what the next planet punishes.
4. Leave. The event you already chose does not reopen.

## What to buy first

- **Resilience** before the acid planet and before any lava cave. A multiplayer tour of six races will shunt you into the hazard whether you planned the line or not.
- **Afterburner** for races you can restart in your head. It deploys whenever the meter is up.
- **Ramjet** only after you can hold a line. It is the stronger boost, and it overheats into an explosion if you stay on it through a corner.
- Leave experimental pieces for a run you are willing to throw. Hibi's own warning is the tell.

She does not have an unlock screen. If the paddock is on screen, she is in it.
