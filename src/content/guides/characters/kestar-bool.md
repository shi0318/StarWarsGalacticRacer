Kestar Bool is the reason the campaign has a finish line. He is the reigning champion, a Caskadag, and the son of the Bool family who turned Pax's league into a family business. Official copy calls him as talented as he is villainous. He threatens other pilots from the chair, not just from the cockpit.

Multiplayer guides list him as the Rank 40 cap of the standard roster, style Weaponised: the speeder is the weapon. That rank is not printed on the store page. Treat it as the reported unlock, then check the in-game rank track before you grind to a number a guide invented.

The voice performance is built to be immediately annoying. That is intentional. If a wreck-out cutscene has you wanting another tour just to shut him up, the scene did its job. Players on the first weekend were already writing reviews about that exact reaction.

## What he controls

Bool does not merely win races. He uses the championship to push the Bool family's reach across the circuit. Shade's grudge is against that family, which is why Pax can recruit her at all. You are not climbing a ranked ladder for its own sake. You are trying to take the title off him before the tour kills the speeder.

## How to approach his stretch of the tour

- Do not arrive at the late events on the bike you used for Jakku. Fragility is a choice he will cash in.
- A run that crashes out "too many times" ends the tour. His events are where that rule stops being theoretical.
- Derven Acos is the showdown the launch trailer points at. Treat the planets before it as build checks, not as the ending.
- He is not an unlockable starter. Beating him is the campaign goal, not a garage slot you buy.

If you only want the race and not the grudge, Arcade and quick multiplayer never require you to sit through his pitch.
