Sebulba is the one classic podracer the launch material puts back in a cockpit. He is not a skin on a landspeeder. Pods run their own events because they are faster and much larger than the three speeder classes, and he lives in that half of the game.

In the release trailer he stops Shade short of Kestar Bool. The point of the scene is practice. He will not wave a new pilot onto a pod and point them at the champion. He also wants his own machine back. Both things can be true.

## When you can actually race a pod

- **Arcade and scenario pod events** are on the menu from the start. You do not have to finish the story to see a pod.
- **Campaign podracing** is a later stretch. Plenty of first-weekend reviews say they still had not unlocked it after several hours, and that matches the way the tour gates vehicle types.
- Online has podracer-focused events. Quick play for the speeder grid is a different queue. Players have asked for a pod quick race; do not assume the speeder quick-race button includes them.

## How to drive one

A pod is two engines and a cockpit between them. The cable is the crash. Leave room, do not treat a ramjet like a landspeeder boost, and expect the first canyon to end the run if you boost into a mouth you have not seen. Tatooine's cave layout is the one that feels closest to the old Mos Espa race, and it is the wrong place to learn the throttle.

Sebulba is a story opponent and a podracing face, not a garage unlock you buy with credits. Beat his stretch, or ignore it and stay in Arcade if that is the only race you wanted.
