Shade is the pilot on the stick. The campaign does not hand you a roster select and ask who you want to be. You are Shade, a lone racer Darius Pax pulls in because she already has a reason to hate the Bool family.

## What she is doing in the League

Kestar Bool took the championship and, with it, the League Pax built. Shade's job on a run is to climb that circuit and unseat him. Hibi keeps the workshop. A podracer the trailers only call "legendary" sits on the other side of that alliance. The story is rivalry and a corrupt circuit, not a Force plot. The store page still says it plainly: no Force, no prophecy.

## How a run actually plays

A tour is a chain of events you choose. Once you pick an event, that choice stays. Crash out too many times and the tour ends. You regroup, rebuild, and start another one. Only some perks and unlocks come with you. That is why the first three planets feel short and the later ones feel like a different game: the build, not the planet list, is what changed.

Players who have finished early tours describe the same loop. You learn the line, you spend the paddock credits at Hibi, and you do not treat a wreck as a reload. It is a failed run.

## What to do on the first tour

- Stay in a landspeeder until the shunt timing feels boring. Bikes punish the same mistake harder.
- Put the first spare credits into resilience before you chase a bigger ramjet. Sentinel One and Lantaana both delete a glass build.
- Do not skip the paddock conversation. Parts you buy there are the only mid-tour power you get.
- Save the podracing stretch for when you want it. Arcade pods are on the menu if you only came for Sebulba.

Shade herself does not unlock. She is the file. The thing you unlock is the garage around her.
