The three named hulls do not have separate showcase shots here. The craft above is a landspeeder, the class the Darc X uses. It is not a photo of the Darc X, Ciza T, or Rak S.


The Deluxe upgrade adds three named hulls, not three new physics classes.

| Hull | Class | What you are really getting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kor Sarun: Darc X | Landspeeder | The tank class, exclusive body |
| Kor Sarun: Ciza T | Speeder bike | Kinetic Burst, exclusive body |
| Kor Sarun: Rak S | Skim speeder | Knife Edge, exclusive body |

They also come with a Naboo N-1 inspired livery you can put on any speeder, three arcade trials built for those hulls, a multiplayer banner, and a digital art book. None of that changes lap time by itself. The trials are the only piece that adds races.

Standard edition is $59.99 on Steam. The upgrade is a separate purchase. If you already own the base game, you do not need these hulls to finish a tour. Buy them if you want the paddock toys, not because a guide told you the Darc X is secretly faster. It is a landspeeder. Drive it like one. See the [landspeeder](/vehicles/landspeeders/), [bike](/vehicles/speeder-bikes/), and [skim speeder](/vehicles/skim-speeders/) pages for the actual class rules.
