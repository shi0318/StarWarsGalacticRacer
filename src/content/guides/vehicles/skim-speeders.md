Skim speeders did not exist in the films. This game introduces them. Official copy puts them between the landspeeder and the speeder bike: flowing lines, less tank than the speeder, less brittle than the bike. The ability you have to learn is the Knife Edge.

## What to practice

A Knife Edge is a committed lean through a long bend, not a panic drift. Forest passes and the river circuits reward it. Canyon mouths and scrap chicanes do not. If the corner is a wall you cannot see until you are in it, you wanted a landspeeder.

## Hazard check

They can cross Sentinel One. They should not cross it on a resilience-zero build. The yellow pools in the store shots are the same hazard players describe on that planet: leave the line, lose the speeder. Knife Edge keeps you on the line. It does not pull you out of acid.

The Deluxe **Kor Sarun: Rak S** is a skim speeder. Same ability, exclusive hull, three arcade trials if you bought the upgrade. The trials do not change the class.
