Landspeeders are the class the launch notes call the tanks. Great acceleration, real top speed, enough resilience to survive a shunt, and a drift that actually rotates the nose. If a tour is going badly, this is the hull you put back on.

They share the grid with speeder bikes and skim speeders. Podracers do not. Do not build a landspeeder expecting to line up against Sebulba. That race is a different event.

## Where they win

- **Sentinel One.** Acid off the racing line ends a fragile bike. The landspeeder's resilience is the difference between a bad apex and a dead tour.
- **Lantaana.** Heat still cooks a ramjet, but you can take a wall and keep the run.
- **Any first tour.** You are learning lines that the game does not mark well. Players keep saying the road reads late. A tank forgives that.

## Where they lose

A bike's Kinetic Burst leaves you on exit if the corner was clean. A skim speeder's Knife Edge holds a flowing line you cannot match by drifting later. On Crait, where the draft itself blinds you, raw durability does not buy the straight. Switch when the lap is about exit speed, not about surviving the pack.

## Build notes

Spend the first Hibi credits on resilience, then afterburner. Ramjet is the stronger boost and the one that explodes the speeder if you hold it through traffic. On a landspeeder you can afford to learn that mistake. On a bike you cannot.

The Deluxe **Kor Sarun: Darc X** is a landspeeder hull, not a new class. If you own it, the advice above still applies.
