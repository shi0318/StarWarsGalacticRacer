Podracers are not a fourth speeder with a bigger engine. The launch announcement is specific: landspeeders, speeder bikes, and skim speeders race each other. Pods race alone, because they are faster and much larger. Put one in the speeder pack and the cable ends somebody's race by accident.

## Unlock

Arcade and scenario pod events are available when you start the game. The campaign's podracing stretch is not. Players on the first weekend were still locked out of story pods after several hours, which matches Sebulba's trailer line: he will not wave Shade at Kestar Bool in a pod without the practice.

If you only want the Mos Espa feeling, open Arcade. Do not grind the speeder tour expecting a pod to appear in that garage.

## Driving

- The engines are the wide part. Aim the gap, not the cockpit.
- Ramjet on a pod is how you hit a cave mouth at full commit. Tap it on the straight, lift before the wall you cannot see.
- There is no useful minimap. Learn the canyon in a slow pass before you boost.
- Online pod events exist. The speeder quick race is a different button. Several players have asked for a pod quick race and do not have one yet.

Sebulba is the face of this grid. The [Sebulba page](/characters/sebulba/) covers where he sits in the story.
