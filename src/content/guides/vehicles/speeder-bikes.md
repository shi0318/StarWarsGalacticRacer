Speeder bikes are the glass class. Fuse Games' launch note is blunt: fast, agile, fragile. They race on the same grid as landspeeders and skim speeders. The thing they have that the others do not is Kinetic Burst.

## Kinetic Burst

Brake into the corner and the bike stores that energy. Release it on exit as a snap of acceleration. It is not the afterburner and it is not the ramjet. Those two are boost systems you fit in the workshop. Kinetic Burst is the class.

Use it on Ando Prime's flowing ice and on forest passes where the exit is clean. Do not try to "store" it by stabbing the brake in a pack. You will arrive at the apex sideways, and a bike does not have the resilience to buy that back.

## When to leave it in the garage

- Acid pools on Sentinel One. One shunt and the tour is over.
- Lava caves on Lantaana if your ramjet habit is still sloppy. Overheating a bike is a wreck, not a spin.
- The first hour. Learn the unmarked line in a landspeeder, then move.

The Deluxe **Kor Sarun: Ciza T** is this class with a different hull. Kinetic Burst still decides whether the bike was worth taking.
