Online is where the class choice stops being a preference.

| Class | Online job | Do not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landspeeder | Survive a six-race tour and the acid planet | Expect to win Crait on durability alone |
| Speeder bike | Clean exits with Kinetic Burst | Take it into Sentinel One on a zero-resilience build |
| Skim speeder | Hold a Knife Edge on flowing circuits | Treat it as a tank |
| Podracer | Its own event | Queue the speeder quick race and wait for one |

Pods are faster and larger, so they do not share the speeder grid. That was true in the launch notes and it is true in the menu. A "best online vehicle" list that ranks a pod against a landspeeder is comparing two races.

Quick race forgives a bad class because the next lobby is a new lobby. The tour does not. Six races, upgrades between them, Derven Acos at the end. Bring the landspeeder unless the earlier planets in that tour were already clean.
