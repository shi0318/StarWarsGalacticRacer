The launch announcement put the game on PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S on October 6, 2026. Steam sells the Windows build for $59.99. That is the list.

## Switch 2

No. A search spike is not a store page. Nothing in the launch note names Nintendo Switch or Switch 2. If a later port is announced, it will be a dated store listing, not a hidden icon in the PS5 build.

## Game Pass

No launch-day Game Pass release was part of the store listing. Xbox Series X|S can own the game. Owning it on Xbox is not the same as it being on Game Pass. Check the Xbox store on the day you care. Do not treat a forum guess as a date.

## What is shared

Cross-play is on across the three platforms that exist. Private lobbies are not. Split-screen is not. Pods and speeders are separate queues on every platform. A setting guide written for a Deck or a Switch does not apply until that hardware has a build.
