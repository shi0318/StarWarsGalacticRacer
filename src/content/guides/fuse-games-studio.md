Fuse Games developed the game. Secret Mode published it, with Lucasfilm Games. It is Fuse's first title, and it shipped on October 6, 2026 after about three and a half years.

The studio is in Guildford. Co-founder and CEO Matt Webster came out of EA and Criterion, with Burnout and Need for Speed on the CV. That shows up in the race, not in a studio history quiz: shunt is a legal move, the ramjet is a new boost that overheats, and the audio is doing as much of the speed as the blur.

You do not need the interview to play. If a review calls it a Burnout game in the Outer Rim, that comparison is about the people who built the contact, not about a license. The systems those people shipped are on the [vehicle pages](/vehicles/) and the [beginner guide](/beginner-guide/).
