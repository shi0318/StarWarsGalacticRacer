There is no roster of 20 secret racers. The unlocks that change a race are smaller than the pre-launch pages guessed.

## Available immediately

- Shade, the campaign, and the speeder garage.
- Landspeeders, speeder bikes, and skim speeders on the mixed grid.
- Arcade and scenario events, including pods.
- Online quick race and the six-race tour.
- Livery, colour, and patina. Cosmetic. Do this last.

## Earned

- **Workshop parts** from Hibi: resilience, afterburner, ramjet, and the experimental pieces she will warn you about. Credits come from events. A wrecked tour does not keep the whole speeder.
- **Campaign pods.** Story podracing is a later stretch. Sebulba will not wave you at Kestar Bool in a pod without the practice. Arcade pods do not wait for that.
- **Some perks** carry into the next tour. Most of the build does not. That is the runs structure, not a bug.

## Paid, not earned

The Kor Sarun Darc X, Ciza T, and Rak S, the N-1 livery, three arcade trials, and a multiplayer banner come from the Deluxe upgrade. They are not a progression track. Details are on the [editions page](/editions/).

## Not an unlock

Kestar Bool, Darius Pax, and Hibi are story roles. You do not buy them. Planets are on the tour, not behind a character level. If a site lists a button code that unlocks every track, it is inventing one. The [cheats page](/cheats/) is the short version of that.
