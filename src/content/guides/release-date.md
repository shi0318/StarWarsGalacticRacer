## When does Star Wars Galactic Racer release?

**Star Wars Galactic Racer is set to launch on October 6, 2026.** As of 2026-07-24 that is the target date. If the developer shifts it, this page updates the same day, and the countdown on our [home page](/) tracks the current date automatically.

## Platforms

The game is planned for:

- **PC** (Steam)
- **PlayStation 5**
- **Xbox Series X|S**
- **Nintendo Switch 2**

Cross-platform play and any platform-exclusive content are **not confirmed** yet. We'll add them here with a source when announced.

## What we know so far

- It's an arcade-style racer set across recognisable Star Wars locations
- The reveal trailer shows **podracing-style twin-engine craft**, desert-canyon and city environments, and a boost/drift mechanic
- A tunable **upgrade bay** appears in marketing, hinting at a component/build system

Everything beyond the release date and platforms is currently based on the trailer or on Star Wars canon, and is flagged as such throughout this wiki. See our [beginner guide](/beginner-guide/) for how the game likely plays, and the [full guide hub](/guide/) for every topic.
