## Is it out?

Yes. **October 6, 2026** was the launch date, and the Steam page for App 4078430 no longer lists the game as coming soon. You can buy it now.

## Platforms and price

- **PC** on Steam, Windows, **$59.99**.
- **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X|S**, confirmed in the launch announcement. Both shipped on October 6.
- Steam's own platform tick is still Windows only. There is no Mac or Linux tab. Player reports say the build runs on SteamOS; that is not the same as an official Linux store listing.

Minimum PC spec on the store page: Windows 10, Core i5-8400 or Ryzen 5 2600, 12 GB RAM, RTX 2060 / Arc A580 / RX 5600 XT 6 GB, 50 GB on an SSD.

## Editions

Standard is the base game. Deluxe adds the three Kor Sarun hulls (Darc X landspeeder, Ciza T speeder bike, Rak S skim speeder), a Naboo N-1 livery, three arcade trials, a multiplayer banner, and a digital art book. Those hulls are exclusive bodies, not new physics classes. Details are on the [Kor Sarun page](/vehicles/kor-sarun/).
