## When does Star Wars Galactic Racer release?

**October 6, 2026.** Fuse Games and Secret Mode announced the date on **30 April 2026**, and the Steam store page for App 4078430 carries the same date in its release field. This has been stable for three months across both first-party channels.

## Platforms: what is confirmed, and what is not

This section needs care, because a lot of sites — including an earlier version of this page — list console platforms that no official source has announced.

**Confirmed:**

- **PC**. The official pre-order post says plainly that the game "will be coming to **PC** on October 6th," and describes the two pre-order editions as being "available to pre-order today **for PC**." One of the pre-order bonuses, an orange Livery, is called out as "**exclusive to PC pre-orders**." Steam reports Windows support.

**Not confirmed by any official source:**

- **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, or Nintendo Switch 2.** No Fuse Games or Secret Mode announcement names any console. We previously listed all three here; that was not sourced, and it has been removed.

Two details make consoles *plausible* without confirming them, and it is worth being precise about the difference. The Steam page carries a **Cross-Platform Multiplayer** category tag, which normally implies more than one platform. And the **Collector's Edition is a physical product** with a steel case, which is unusual for a PC-only release. Neither of those is an announcement. If consoles are announced, this page gets the platform list and the source on the same day.

## Price and editions

The Steam store page lists **$59.99** for the standard edition. Three editions have been officially detailed:

- **Standard Edition** — the base game.
- **Deluxe Edition** — adds three Kor Sarun repulsorcraft, three Deluxe Arcade Events, the Deluxe Livery Pack, a Player Banner Pack, and an 80-page digital art book.
- **Collector's Edition** — the Deluxe digital content plus physical items: a metal model of the Kor Sarun Darc X landspeeder, Kestar's Banner bearing the Bool Family Sigil, racing pilot patches for Shade and a rival racer, a printed art book, and a steel case.

Full contents are broken down on our [editions page](/editions/). Pre-order bonuses for both digital editions include a bonus Livery and a multiplayer Player Banner.

## What else is officially confirmed about the launch build

- **NVIDIA DLSS 4.5** is confirmed to be in the game at launch, per a first-party post on 10 March 2026.
- **Online multiplayer** is confirmed, alongside the story-driven solo campaign. Steam lists PvP and Online PvP categories.
- **Four vehicle classes** — landspeeders, speeder bikes, skim speeders, and podracers.

## Still unannounced

- Console platforms and any console release timing.
- Preload size and dates, and per-region unlock times.
- Whether cross-platform play is enabled between specific platforms, despite the category tag.
- Post-launch content plans.
