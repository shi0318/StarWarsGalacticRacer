## Campaign

You are Shade. You pick events, the pick stays, and too many crashes end the tour. Hibi fits parts between races. The late target is Kestar Bool, and Derven Acos is the arena that stacks the planets you already survived. Full notes are on the [run guide](/campaign-run-planning/).

## Arcade and scenarios

Arcade is the practice menu: time trials, precision events, and **pods from the first minute**. You do not need the story gate. Deluxe adds three trials for the Kor Sarun hulls.

Scenarios are the named challenges with a specific goal, separate from a plain time trial. They shipped with the game. They are not a secret post-launch mode.

## Online

- Up to **12 players**.
- **Quick race** is the playlist people are actually finishing.
- The **tour** is six races, upgrades between them, showdown on Derven Acos.
- **Pod events** are their own queue. The speeder quick race does not include them.
- Cross-play is on across PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.
- Private lobbies and a custom friend race are not in the launch build.

Contact is intended. This is a combat racer. A clean line still loses to a shunt if your resilience is zero. That tradeoff is on the [build page](/tips/campaign-builds/) and the [multiplayer page](/tips/multiplayer-tour/).
