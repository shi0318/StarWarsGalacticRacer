Fuse Games has confirmed a spread of modes across two official trailers. This page separates what has been announced from what is still vague. It is drawn from first-party trailers only.

## Solo campaign (runs-based)

The headline mode. You play as **Shade**, working up through the **Galactic League** to unseat its corrupt champion, Kestar Bool. It is structured as a **runs-based** campaign: you pick between branching events, choices are permanent, and crashing out too often forces a fresh run with only some perks carried over.

This is the mode with the most confirmed detail, and it has its own page — see [story and campaign](/story-campaign/) for the full structure, the paddock, and Hibi's workshop.

## Arcade mode

Arcade is where you go for skill-focused, bite-sized racing outside the story:

- **Time trials** — race the clock.
- **Precision driving challenges** — hit specific targets and lines.
- **Podracing from the start** — you do not have to reach a campaign milestone to podrace. Arcade lets you jump straight into podracers as soon as you begin playing.

The **Deluxe Edition** adds three Arcade events built specifically around its exclusive Kor Sarun vehicles, with target times to beat. Those are covered on our [editions page](/editions/).

## Scenarios mode

Scenarios is described as a set of **unique events and challenges** that test your racing against specific goals — distinct from Arcade's time-and-precision focus. Fuse Games has not detailed individual scenarios yet, so treat this as a confirmed mode with unconfirmed contents.

## Online multiplayer

Confirmed details:

- **Up to 12 players** per race.
- A progression layer: "climb the ranks, challenge rivals, and shape your path as an elite racer."
- **Podracer-focused online events** — called out separately in the Story Trailer as a first for the series.
- Press coverage from Summer Game Fest highlighted a **"grudge match"** mode as a standout, consistent with the campaign's rivalry framing.

Aggressive contact is part of the design. The creative director, an ex-Criterion (Burnout) developer, confirmed that "rubbin'" is viable: "It's okay to give people a bit of a smash." So expect multiplayer to reward takedowns, not just clean lines.

## What is not confirmed

- Whether multiplayer is ranked-only or has casual playlists.
- Split-screen or local multiplayer support.
- Cross-platform play.
- The number of Scenarios, and whether they expand post-launch.
