Fuse Games and Secret Mode have announced **three editions**: a digital Standard, a digital Deluxe, and a physical Collector's Edition. This page lays out exactly what each one includes, straight from the official pre-order announcements. Prices are not itemised in those posts, so we do not guess at them — check the store listing.

## Pre-order bonuses (all editions)

Pre-ordering either digital edition on PC grants:

- A bonus **Livery for your repulsorcraft**, usable on landspeeders, speeder bikes, and skim speeders. The **orange Livery is exclusive to PC pre-orders**.
- A bonus **Player Banner** for multiplayer.

## Standard Edition

The base game plus the pre-order bonuses above. That is the whole package — no extra vehicles or events.

## Deluxe Edition

The base game, the pre-order bonuses, and a bundle of extra digital content built around three exclusive **Kor Sarun** vehicles:

| Deluxe content | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Kor Sarun: Darc X** | Exclusive landspeeder |
| **Kor Sarun: Ciza T** | Exclusive speeder bike |
| **Kor Sarun: Rac S** | Exclusive skim speeder |
| **Deluxe Arcade Events** | Three Arcade events built for the vehicles above, with target times |
| **Deluxe Livery Pack** | Naboo-starfighter-inspired designs for any speeder |
| **Deluxe Player Banner Pack** | An exclusive Insignia and Background for multiplayer |
| **Digital Art Book** | 80 pages of character, vehicle, and location artwork |

Important for the value question: **all of the Deluxe content will also be sold separately as the "Deluxe Upgrade" DLC at launch.** So buying Standard now does not lock you out of it later — you can add it whenever.

## Collector's Edition

The physical package. It includes **everything in the Deluxe Edition digitally**, plus these physical items:

- A finely crafted **metal model of the Kor Sarun: Darc X landspeeder**.
- **Kestar's Banner**, featuring the Bool family sigil.
- Racing pilot **patches** for Shade and a rival racer.
- A physical **80-page art book** in high-quality print.
- A **steel case** with a custom slipcover using the Deluxe key art.
- All packaged in a premium Collector's Edition box.

## Which should you pick?

- **Just want the game:** Standard. You can add the Deluxe Upgrade later if you change your mind.
- **Want the extra vehicles and events day one, digital only:** Deluxe.
- **Star Wars collector who wants the metal Darc X and art book on a shelf:** Collector's Edition, which folds in the Deluxe digital content anyway.

One caveat worth stating: the three Kor Sarun vehicles are **cosmetic-plus** — they come with dedicated Arcade events, but Fuse Games has not said they are faster than base-game craft. Do not buy Deluxe expecting a competitive multiplayer advantage; buy it for the content. For how vehicle classes actually differ, see the [fastest vehicle guide](/tips/fastest-vehicle/).
