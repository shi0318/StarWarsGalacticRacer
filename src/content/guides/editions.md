Standard edition on Steam is **$59.99**. That is the game. Deluxe is an upgrade, not a different physics build.

## Deluxe upgrade

| Piece | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Kor Sarun: Darc X | Exclusive landspeeder hull |
| Kor Sarun: Ciza T | Exclusive speeder bike |
| Kor Sarun: Rak S | Exclusive skim speeder |
| N-1 livery | Naboo-starfighter paint for any speeder |
| Three arcade trials | Events built for those hulls |
| Banner | Multiplayer insignia and background |
| Art book | Digital, development art |

The three hulls use the class they belong to. Darc X is still a landspeeder. Ciza T still has Kinetic Burst. Rak S still needs a Knife Edge. Buying Deluxe does not put you ahead of a Standard player who spent credits on resilience. The trials are the only extra races.

Collector's Edition is the physical box: a metal Darc X, Kestar's banner, patches, a printed art book, and a steel case, plus the Deluxe digital content. It does not add a track.

Hull-by-hull notes are on the [Kor Sarun page](/vehicles/kor-sarun/). Class rules are on the [vehicle list](/vehicles/).
