The game shipped on **October 6, 2026**. PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. Steam sells the Windows version for **$59.99**. It is not free, and it is not still on a countdown.

You play Shade. Hibi fits the speeder. Darius Pax founded the League. Kestar Bool took it. That is the campaign. The rest of the menu is there so you can ignore the story when you want to.

## Modes

- **Campaign.** A run of events you choose and cannot unchoose. Crash out too often and the tour ends. Some unlocks carry. Most of the build does not.
- **Arcade and scenario events.** The place to practice a planet, and the place pods are available immediately.
- **Online.** Quick races, a six-race upgrade tour that ends on Derven Acos, and separate pod events. Cross-play is on. Private lobbies are not.

## The garage, in one paragraph

Three speeder classes share a grid. Landspeeders are the tanks. Speeder bikes are fast and fragile, with Kinetic Burst. Skim speeders sit between them and use a Knife Edge. Podracers race alone because they are faster and larger. Two boosts exist on top of that: afterburner, which you can tap, and ramjet, which you must hold and which will explode the craft if you do not lift.

## PC spec

Steam's minimum is Windows 10, a Core i5-8400 or Ryzen 5 2600, **12 GB RAM**, an RTX 2060 / Arc A580 / RX 5600 XT 6 GB, and **50 GB on an SSD**. Recommended steps up to 16 GB and an RTX 3060 Ti, Arc B580, or RX 6700 XT. The SSD line is not optional in the store text.

Linux players have reported the Steam Deck / SteamOS build running cleanly at launch. That is a player report, not a Linux store tab. The Steam page itself is still Windows-only.
