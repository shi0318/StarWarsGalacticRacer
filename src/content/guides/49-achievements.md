![A ruins circuit in STAR WARS: Galactic Racer](/images/swgr-ruins-circuit.webp)

Steam App 4078430 lists **49 achievements**. That is the completion ceiling on October 16, 2026. The storefront does not publish a missable table, so this page will not invent gold times or a hidden cup order.

Arcade targets stay on the event screen. Planet pages stay on [tracks](/tracks/). The campaign branches. It does not number the seven settings into a single required order.

## A practical order

- Finish a campaign tour before you chase multiplayer pops. See [how long a tour takes](/how-long-to-beat/).
- Shunts and takedowns are combat, not achievement recipes. See [shunts and takedowns](/tips/shunt-takedown/).
- If a pop fails after the driver hotfix, restart into that build and rerun the same event before you assume it is missable.
