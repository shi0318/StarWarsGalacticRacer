This page now points at the class page. Use that one.

Landspeeders shipped as the durable option: acceleration, top speed, a drift that rotates, and enough resilience to take a shunt. They share the grid with bikes and skim speeders. They do not line up against pods.

Take one for Jakku, Sentinel One, Lantaana, and any tour you are not willing to throw. Switch away on Crait if the problem is draft blindness rather than contact. The Deluxe Kor Sarun: Darc X is this class with a different hull.

The full notes, including the build order around them, are on the [landspeeder class page](/vehicles/landspeeders/).
