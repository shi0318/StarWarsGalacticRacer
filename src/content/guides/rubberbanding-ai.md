An 8-hour negative file calls campaign rubberbanding so bad it stops being a racing game: a slot machine with racing as the medium. A 32-hour positive file still plays, and still hates scripted overtakes. Those are campaign bots. They are not the public lobby.

Online is the other complaint: bumper cars, balloon health, no repair between races except when you arrive on a planet. A bad race that wrecks the speeder cannot be paid off in the paddock. That is a tour rule, not a rubber-band slider. Private lobbies still do not exist. See [no private lobbies](/tips/no-private-lobbies/).

## Split the two races

- Campaign: expect the pack to catch a clean line. Resilience before acid planets still matters. See the [beginner guide](/beginner-guide/).
- Online tour: six races, shunts, a thinner speeder when you wreck. Repair is at planet arrival, not mid-card.
- Takedown cam putting you in a wall is a third issue. See [takedown camera](/tips/takedown-camera/).
- Missing keybinds are a fourth. See [Steam features](/steam-features-controller/).

Champion difficulty unlocks after you beat the game. It does not turn the bots off. It is a harder tour on the same rubber band.
