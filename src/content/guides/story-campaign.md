Darius Pax founded the Galactic League. Kestar Bool, the champion, took it and uses the title to push the Bool family further. Pax recruits Shade because she already has a grudge against that family. Hibi keeps the speeder alive between events. A podracer the trailers only call legendary sits on the same side of the table, and Sebulba is the one who refuses to let a new pilot at Bool in a pod without practice.

There is no Force plot. The store page still says it: no Force, no prophecy, skill and strategy.

## How the story is played

It is a run, not a chapter select. You choose events and cannot unchoose them. Crash out too often and the tour ends. Some perks carry. You start the next attempt with a thinner garage than you finished on. Derven Acos is the custom arena that combines the planets' conditions into one gauntlet. Multiplayer runs end in a showdown there too.
