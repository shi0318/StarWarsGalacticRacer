Buy it if you want a combat racer with a run structure: pick an event, live with it, wreck out, come back thinner. That is the game that launched on October 6, 2026, at $59.99 on Steam for the standard edition. PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S all shipped. A sale does not change the shape.

## Buy it if

- You like a parry-free fight where the shunt is legal and the ramjet can explode the speeder.
- You want pods, but you can accept they are a separate grid and a later campaign stretch.
- You are fine with no private lobby and thin in-game rebinding. Those were launch complaints. They are still the missing pieces.

## Skip it if

- You want a 20-course career mode with a ranked ladder and a friend lobby. The planet list is short. The tour is the content. Track-count complaints in the first weekend were fair.
- You want codes, a chapter select, or a Switch 2 build. None of those shipped. The [platforms page](/platforms/) is the short answer.
- You want Deluxe because a page called a hull faster. The Kor Sarun bodies are the same three classes.

The [editions page](/editions/) is the money. This page is the fit.
