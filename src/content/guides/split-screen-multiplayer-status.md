Local split-screen did not ship. The feature list that did ship is single-player, online PvP, and cross-platform multiplayer on PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. No store category and no launch note adds a second local player.

If you wanted a friend on the couch, the working version is two copies and an online quick race. Even that is a public queue. Private lobbies and a custom friend race are the other missing piece, and players have already asked for both. They are not hidden in a menu.

The queues that do exist are on the [cross-play page](/cross-platform-pvp-status/).
