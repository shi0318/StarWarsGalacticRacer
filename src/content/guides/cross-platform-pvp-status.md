The official Steam page for **STAR WARS: Galactic Racer** now lists **Multi-player**, **PvP**, **Online PvP**, and **Cross-Platform Multiplayer** alongside Single-player. That is the strongest first-party confirmation yet that online racing is not limited to one storefront or platform.

## What the official categories confirm

| Steam category | Confirmed meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Multi-player** | The game has a multiplayer component. |
| **PvP** | Player-versus-player competition is part of the listed feature set. |
| **Online PvP** | PvP is listed for online play. |
| **Cross-Platform Multiplayer** | Steam currently marks multiplayer as cross-platform. |
| **Single-player** | The story-driven solo side remains a listed feature too. |

This matches the store description's competitive framing: it mentions strategic PvP grudge matches and a solo campaign. It also replaces the older assumption that cross-platform play had not been announced.

## What is still not specified

The listing does not give the information needed to plan a specific group session:

- the supported platform combinations;
- whether cross-platform play can be disabled;
- party, invite, voice-chat, and matchmaking rules;
- ranked queues, regions, anti-cheat, or input-based matchmaking;
- cross-progression and cross-save;
- local split-screen or couch multiplayer.

Do not use the Cross-Platform Multiplayer tag to claim any of those details. They require a platform matrix or a multiplayer FAQ from Fuse Games or Secret Mode.

## A sensible launch checklist

1. Check the final in-game account and privacy settings before joining an online race.
2. Confirm which platforms your group owns and whether the release build permits that exact pairing.
3. Start with an unranked or low-stakes session if the final mode list separates those options.
4. Record the build version and region when reporting a matchmaking or party problem.
