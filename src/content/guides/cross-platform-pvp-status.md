Cross-play shipped. The launch announcement put the game on PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, and the Steam page lists Cross-Platform Multiplayer, Online PvP, and PvP. You are not in a PC-only lobby.

## What you can queue

- **Quick race.** The mode players currently prefer. Show up, race, leave.
- **Six-race tour.** Upgrades between races, then a showdown on Derven Acos. This is the campaign loop with strangers shunting you.
- **Pod events.** Separate from the speeder quick race. A plain pod quick-race button is something players are still asking for. Do not assume the speeder queue includes Sebulba's grid.

## What you cannot do

Private lobbies and a custom race for you and a friend are not in the launch build. In-game rebinding is thin, and the walking paddock has no field-of-view slider. Those are the three requests that show up in the first weekend of reviews. Plan around them. Do not sit in a menu waiting for a private room.

Split-screen is a different question, covered on the [split-screen page](/split-screen-multiplayer-status/). The tour itself is on the [multiplayer page](/tips/multiplayer-tour/).
