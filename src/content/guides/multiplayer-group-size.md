The loud first-week complaint was party size. Fuse Games said the multiplayer group limit will double in a future update. That is a plan, not a hidden slider in the October 6 build. Cross-platform PvP already shipped. Private lobbies did not. Split-screen did not.

Until that update, the tour is still the content: six races, shunts, a speeder that gets thinner when you wreck out. Online is extra spice on a short planet list. Do not wait in a menu for eight names if the current cap is four. Play the tour. Come back when the note ships.

## What to do this week

- Treat the current cap as the live rule. A doubling is a patch, not a setting you missed.
- Cross-play status lives on [cross-platform PvP](/cross-platform-pvp-status/). Split-screen status lives on [split-screen](/split-screen-multiplayer-status/).
- If acid-planet races are deleting you, resilience before the gate is the build note, not party size. See the [beginner guide](/beginner-guide/).
- Very Positive at 3,406 of 4,011 is the combat racer. It is not a promise that your eight-person Discord is in this build.

The [worth-it page](/is-it-worth-it/) is still the buy test. This page is the one complaint the studio already said they will move.
