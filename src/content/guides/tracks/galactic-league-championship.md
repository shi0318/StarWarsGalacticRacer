**The Galactic League Championship** is the longest track in Star Wars Galactic Racer. One lap is **35.7 km**, the race is **2 laps**, and it is **podracer only**. It runs on **Derven Acos**, the Galactic League's custom arena, and it is the podracing counterpart to [The Galactic Gauntlet](/tracks/the-galactic-gauntlet/).

The Championship is **half as long again as the Gauntlet** and climbs more than **twice as much per lap**. Compared with [Mos Espa Speedway](/tracks/mos-espa-speedway/), the classic flat pod circuit, its elevation range is over ten times larger.

## What that means for the race

- **It is a long race.** About 71 km over two laps, with **eight checkpoints**. A crash in the first half does not end it; a crash on the second lap often does.
- **Wide but few lines.** At **89 m** it is the widest track covered here, but it has only **seven alternative lines**. That is a lot of room on one road rather than many roads. Pass on width, not on shortcuts.
- **Heat and boost over distance.** Over 2,000 m of climbing per lap means long uphill stretches. Pods that boost on every climb arrive at the second lap with nothing left. Pick the climbs that matter.
- **No mines, no reverse.** There is only one configuration. Whatever you learn on the first lap holds on the second.

## Where to race it

The only arcade event on the track is **Stadium Spectacular**, a time trial. Use it to learn the 35.7 km lap before you meet the Championship in the league. The time trial removes the pack, so you can learn where the climbs are without someone in your line.
