Sentinel One is the planet the first weekend of advice keeps naming. The hazard is acid off the racing line. Yellow pools sit in the rock. A shunt that would be a spin on Jakku is a dead speeder here.

## Before you queue it

Put points in resilience. That is not a vibe. It is the difference between finishing the six-race multiplayer tour and restarting it. A speeder bike with a pretty Kinetic Burst still loses if the pack puts you in the pool.

## On the lap

- Hold the center until you have seen where the pools actually sit. The store shot puts them beside the line, not across it.
- Do not ramjet through a bend you cannot see. The explosion from an overheat and the explosion from acid look the same from the garage.
- Skim speeders can Knife Edge the flowing sections. They are not immune. Landspeeders are the safe pick for a tour you care about.

If a friend tells you the acid planet is optional, they have not reached the late tour. It shows up. Build for it on Jakku, not on the grid.
