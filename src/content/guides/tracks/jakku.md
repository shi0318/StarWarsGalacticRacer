Jakku is the desert you hit early, and players name it as a repeat tour stop. A crashed Star Destroyer sits in the dunes. The racing line runs through scrap, not around a clean ribbon of tarmac.

## What the lap punishes

There is no useful minimap, and the road does not shout at you. Chinese and English reviews from the first weekend both land on the same complaint: you boost, then you discover the wall. On Jakku that wall is often a piece of wreckage you could have seen if you had lifted.

Treat the first pass as reconnaissance.

- Stay off the ramjet until you have seen the chicane twice.
- Dust hides the apex. If the pack is kicking up a wall of it, move wide rather than guessing.
- A landspeeder survives the lesson. A bike turns the same mistake into a wrecked tour.

## Build

Resilience is optional here and mandatory two planets later. If this is tour one, buy the resilience anyway. You will not get a clean shop reset when Sentinel One shows up.

Jakku is a speeder planet in the early tours. Pods have their own canyon events; do not expect Sebulba's grid on this dune.
