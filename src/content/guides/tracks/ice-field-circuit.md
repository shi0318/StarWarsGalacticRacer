**Ice Field Circuit** is the most-used track on **Ando Prime**. It is a **10.9 km circuit** run over **2 laps**, open to **any vehicle**, and it has more configurations than any other track covered here: **ten**, across forward, reverse, podracer, Shakedown and **mined** versions. Nine separate events use it.

Every layout runs **2 laps**, with an elevation range of about **300 m**.

## What the numbers say

- **Reverse is shorter.** Main · Reverse is **8.7 km**, more than 2 km shorter than forward, so a reverse lap time is not a slower forward lap. Compare like with like.
- **Reverse has far fewer lines.** Forward has **25** alternative lines; reverse has **9**. Forward is a track you can improvise on. Reverse is one you have to know.
- **Special A is a corridor.** About **20 m wide** with **zero or one** alternative line. It is Shakedown-only, and there is no room to recover from a slide on ice.
- **Mines change the main layouts.** Both Main layouts have a mined Standard version. Same route and same length, but the racing line is no longer safe by default.

## Driving on Ando Prime ice

1. **Learn the heat sections before boosting.** Ando Prime mixes ice with heat tunnels. Boosting into a tunnel you are already hot in ends the run. See [Ando Prime](/tracks/ando-prime/).
2. **Start on a landspeeder.** It forgives a late correction on low grip. Move to a bike once your exits are clean.
3. **Practise the reverse layout separately.** With only nine lines and a shorter lap, the reverse version punishes a forward-learned racing line.
4. **For Ramjet Shakedown, distance beats speed.** Read [ramjet heat](/tips/ramjet-heat/) before you try it.
