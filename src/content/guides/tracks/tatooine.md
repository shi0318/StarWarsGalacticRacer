Tatooine is the pod planet. The store shots put classic orange pods in a canyon and a cave, grandstands cut into the rock, exactly the shape of the old Mos Espa race. The story trailer also shows pods leaving that circuit, so do not treat every pod event as "the Tatooine level." This page is the canyon.

## How it drives

Pods are wider than they look from the cockpit. The cave mouth in the shots is the crash. Boost on the straight, lift before the dark, and do not follow a rival into a gap their engines already filled.

## When you can race it

Arcade pod events are available immediately. The campaign version sits behind the podracing stretch, which Sebulba gates with a practice demand. If the speeder garage is all you can see, you are not missing a hidden Tatooine button. Open Arcade.

There is still no minimap. Run the canyon once without the ramjet. The second pass is the race.
