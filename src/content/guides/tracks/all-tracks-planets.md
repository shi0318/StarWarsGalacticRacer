There are seven named launch settings in the current track record. The campaign is a branching run, so the first six do not form one mandatory chapter order. **Derven Acos** is the custom Galactic League arena used as the finale and multiplayer-tour showdown. Use this page as the map, then open the individual route page before spending credits.

## Launch track list

| Setting | Grid | Main hazard | Class planning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Jakku](/tracks/jakku/) | Mixed speeder | Wreckage chicanes and dust | A landspeeder forgives a late correction |
| [Tatooine](/tracks/tatooine/) | Pod grid | Cave mouths and tight canyon walls | Keep a separate podracer line; pods do not share the speeder grid |
| [Ando Prime](/tracks/ando-prime/) | Mixed speeder | Ice, heat tunnels, and cliff drops | Learn the warm sections before boosting |

| [Sentinel One](/tracks/sentinel-one/) | Mixed speeder | Acid pools and low visibility | Resilience matters more than a paper top-speed number |
| [Lantaana](/tracks/lantaana/) | Mixed speeder | Lava caves, heat, and river crossings | Do not hold ramjet through the cave |
| [Derven Acos](/tracks/derven-acos/) | League finale | Several planetary conditions together | Bring the build that survived the earlier tour |

## How the map affects a run

Jakku is the forgiving line-learning stop: wide dunes still hide scrap, so an early boost can end in a wall. Tatooine changes the vehicle question entirely because the pod grid is separate. Ando Prime, Sentinel One, and Lantaana punish a build that only bought speed; heat, ice, acid, and cliff edges are the actual lap conditions. Crait is less about a single hazard than knowing when to leave the draft.

The route is not a fixed chapter list. Event choices stay chosen for that tour, and a wreck-out starts another run with only selected perks carrying over. That is why the [campaign run guide](/campaign-run-planning/) recommends spending for the next environment rather than chasing a universal best vehicle.

## Preparing for Derven Acos

Derven Acos combines the conditions learned on the planetary tracks. Before selecting the final event:

1. Keep resilience above a cosmetic upgrade.
2. Carry a boost part you can control on both a straight and a heat section.
3. Check that the chosen class matches the grid; a pod build cannot be evaluated against the mixed speeder roster.
4. Leave Hibi’s paddock with credits for a recovery part if the tour has already taken a hit.

Individual pages hold the hazard notes and class advice. The [tracks hub](/tracks/) is the index for every setting, while [multiplayer tour tips](/tips/multiplayer-tour/) explains the six-race online version and its Derven Acos finish.
