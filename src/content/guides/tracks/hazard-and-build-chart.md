Seven settings, seven different ways to end a run. This is the reference sheet: what the planet does to you, which class handles it, and whether the ramjet is allowed.

## The chart

| Planet | Hazard | Bring | Boost rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Jakku** — wreck fields | Wreckage chicanes, dust hiding the line, long dunes that punish an early boost | Landspeeder | Afterburner. Dunes punish a boost you spend too early |
| **Ando Prime** — ice runs | Ice and low grip, heat tunnels you have to hit, cliff drops beside the line | Landspeeder, then bike once exits are clean | Afterburner. You are already carrying tunnel heat |
| **Crait** — salt flats | Draft blindness, long exposed straights, stadium lighting at night | Bike or skim speeder | Ramjet on the straights; leave the wake before you spend it |
| **Sentinel One** — acid pools | Acid off the racing line, low visibility, shunts that end the run | Landspeeder | Afterburner. Visibility is the problem, not top speed |
| **Lantaana** — lava caves | Lava caves, heat buildup, river crossings on the surface layout | Landspeeder | Avoid ramjet in the caves entirely |
| **Tatooine** — canyon pods | Cave mouths, tight canyon walls, pod cables crossing the pack | Podracer (its own grid) | Pod rules; do not copy a speeder contact line here |
| **Derven Acos** — the arena | Combined planetary conditions, finale pressure | Whatever survived the list | Everything you learned above, at once |

## How to read it

**Durability beats speed on four of the seven.** Jakku, Ando Prime, Sentinel One and Lantaana all kill you with the environment rather than with the clock. The landspeeder is not a beginner choice on those planets — it is the correct choice.

**Crait is the exception.** The hazard there is visibility inside a draft, not durability, which is the one circuit where the fragile fast classes are the right answer.

**Tatooine is a different game.** Pods race on their own grid at breakneck speed with a much larger footprint. Nothing you learned about shunting a landspeeder transfers.

**Derven Acos is the audit.** It stacks the tour's conditions into one gauntlet, which is why a build tuned for exactly one planet fails there. If the chart above has you switching class every planet, the finale is the race where you find out which switch you got wrong.

## Before the first lap on any of them

Run **one clean lap with no contact**. Note the braking marker and the hazard. Contact is lap two. Adding a shunt before you can finish the lap clean is how a run ends on the hazard rather than on the rival.
