Ando Prime is the snow planet players describe as a heat puzzle. Stay in the open ice too long and you freeze. The lit tunnels and orange gates in the store shots are the warm sections. Hit them. Do not treat them as scenery.

## The line

Grip is the other half. A late drift on ice is a slide into the cliff, and the cliff is in the shot: a vertical drop right beside the racing line. Speeder bikes are tempting here because Kinetic Burst loves a clean exit. They are also the class that dies when the exit is not clean.

<figure>
  <img src="/images/guide/cliff-drop.webp" alt="A drop beside a snowfield" width="1400" height="788" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

- Aim for the heat tunnel before you aim for the boost pad.
- Landspeeders forgive the first bad slide. Use one until the gate order is memorized.
- A skim speeder's Knife Edge works on the long ridge. It does not save a missed tunnel.

Crait blinds you for drafting. Ando Prime punishes you for staying cold. They are not the same "white planet."
