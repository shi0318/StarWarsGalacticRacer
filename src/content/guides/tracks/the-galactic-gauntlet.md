**The Galactic Gauntlet** is the main circuit on **Derven Acos**, the custom Galactic League arena that hosts the finale. It is the second-longest lap in the game at **23.9 km**, runs **2 laps**, and is open to **any vehicle class**. If you have a speeder build, this is the Derven Acos track you will spend the most time on.

## Two very different tracks with one name

The **main circuit** is wide (67 m on average) and has **29 alternative lines**, more than any other track covered on this site. Losing the racing line costs little, because there is almost always another one. The race is decided by who keeps boost through the climbs: over **1,000 m of climbing per lap** across 23.9 km.

The **Special** layout is the opposite. It is a point-to-point Shakedown run at **21 m wide** with a **single alternative line**. That is a third of the main circuit's width, with nowhere to recover. Treat it as a precision run, not a race.

**Coliseum Blitz** is the cleanest way to learn the lap: no pack, and the time trial rewards the climbs you will meet again in the finale.

## Driving the Gauntlet

1. **Spend boost on the climbs, not the descents.** Gravity already helps downhill; boost uphill is where you gain time on a track that climbs over a kilometre per lap.
2. **Do not fight for one line.** With 29 options, the clean line beats the short line as soon as there is traffic on it.
3. **Bring the build that survived the tour.** Derven Acos mixes the conditions of the earlier planets. Resilience still matters more than a top-speed number. See [Derven Acos](/tracks/derven-acos/).
