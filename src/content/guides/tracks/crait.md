Crait is the white open circuit, and the hazard is other pilots. Players who have run it describe draft blindness: sit in a rival's wake and you stop seeing the straight. That is the opposite of every arcade racer that taught you to glue yourself to the leader.

## How to take it

- Draft for the speed, then leave before the next bend. The wake is a resource with a timer.
- The night stadium layout is a long lit straight into a grandstand. Do not discover the corner at ramjet speed.
- Durability matters less here than on Sentinel One. A bike can win Crait if you can see. It cannot win a lap you are driving blind.

Ando Prime is the other pale planet. There you are hunting heat tunnels. Here you are hunting clean air. If a guide tells you to draft the whole lap, it has these two mixed up.
