Lantaana shows up in player notes as the lava-cave planet, spelled that way, not "Lantana." The surface shots match: a river, a volcano, green banks, and a pack that is already in the water. The caves are the part that ends runs.

## Heat

Ramjet is the strong boost and the one that overheats. On a cool dune you can ride the warning. In a lava cave the warning is late. Lift before the meter cooks the speeder. Afterburner is the boost you can actually use in the dark, because it does not demand a continuous hold.

Players describe the lap as heat management, the same way Ando Prime is cold management. They are a pair. A build that only works in one climate fails the tour.

## Class

Landspeeders again. You will hit a wall you did not read. The river layout gives skim speeders a Knife Edge moment on the banks, then takes it away when the cave narrows. Do not start your first Lantaana tour on a bike.
