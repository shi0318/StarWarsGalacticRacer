## Step 3: Build a transition routine
Before the campaign event begins, take a moment to recognize that you are changing craft and expectations. Read the starting information and let the opening section establish the vehicle's response. Avoid committing immediately to the exact line or timing you used in the preceding race merely because that rhythm still feels natural. A deliberate transition can prevent the first mistake from turning into a series of increasingly aggressive corrections while you try to recover the previous vehicle's feel.

Pick one driving priority for the opening stretch: maintain a stable line, leave room around obstacles, or avoid an unnecessary contact. This is practice advice, not a verified optimal racing sequence. Once control feels predictable, return attention to the event objective and the traffic around you. Trying to fix every corner at once can make a short Podrace feel more chaotic than it needs to be. A simpler routine gives you a way to assess improvement even if you do not enjoy the interruption itself.
