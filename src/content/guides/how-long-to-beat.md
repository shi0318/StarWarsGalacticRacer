A first campaign tour is an evening if you skip what patch 1.0.11 allows and you do not wreck out. It is a weekend if you treat every crash as a reload of the same glass bike. Those are not the same game, so a single "11 hours" plucked from a database is not a fact this page will repeat.

## What actually spends the time

- **The first tour.** Jakku through the later planets, including Sentinel One and Lantaana, plus Derven Acos if you reach it. Learning unmarked lines is the slow part. The [beginner guide](/beginner-guide/) is that hour.
- **Retries.** A wreck-out ends the tour. Some perks carry. Most of the speeder does not. People quoting a short time finished. People quoting a long time restarted.
- **Arcade and pods.** Gold times and the pod stretch are extra. They are not inside the campaign clock. Sebulba's grid is optional if you only want the speeder story, and mandatory if the trophy list says so.
- **Online.** A six-race tour is one sitting. It is not progress toward the campaign ending.

Do not add the clocks together and call the sum the length. Finish the tour you care about, then decide if the list is worth a second one.
