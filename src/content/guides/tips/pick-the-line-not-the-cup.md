![A ruins circuit in STAR WARS: Galactic Racer](/images/swgr-ruins-circuit.webp)

The seven settings are a choice, not a ladder. Jakku, Tatooine, Ando Prime, Crait, Sentinel One, Lantaana, and the Derven Acos finale do not form a numbered cup. Derven Acos is the finale. It is not a separate ruleset you unlock by gold-starring everything else first.

Arcade gold times stay on the event screen. They are not listed here because the screen is the authority, and a copied time goes stale.

## How to pick the next race

- Read the hazard on the [planet page](/tracks/), then the vehicle that survives it.
- If the tour branches, take the branch you can finish clean. A shunt plan from the previous planet does not transfer to an ice gate.
- The full choice structure is on [all tracks and planets](/tracks/all-tracks-planets/). Use that page when a guide elsewhere invents chapter 3 of 12.
