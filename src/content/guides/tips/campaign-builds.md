A tour ends when you crash out too many times. The build is how you stop that happening on Sentinel One instead of on Jakku. Hibi sells the parts. This is the order that matches what the planets actually do.

## Buy this first

1. **Resilience.** Acid pools and lava caves do not care about your lap time. A multiplayer tour is six races of other people shunting you into those hazards.
2. **Afterburner.** Weaker than ramjet, usable whenever the meter is up. This is the boost you keep for corners.
3. **Ramjet.** Stronger, and it has to be held. Lift, or it overheats and the speeder explodes. Do not buy this as your first upgrade and then learn the line with it.

Livery, patina, and the Naboo N-1 paint are free to look at and useless in a pool of acid. Paint the hull after the tour survives.

## Class by planet

| Planet | Bring | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jakku | Landspeeder | Learn the unmarked wrecks without ending the tour |
| Ando Prime | Landspeeder, then bike | Heat tunnels first, Kinetic Burst once the exits are clean |
| Crait | Bike or skim speeder | Draft blindness, not durability, is the hazard |
| Sentinel One | Landspeeder | Acid off line |
| Lantaana | Landspeeder | Ramjet heat in lava caves |
| Derven Acos | Whatever survived the list | The arena stacks those checks |

Podracers are a separate event. Do not "main" one for the speeder tour. Open Arcade when you want that grid.

## Two boosts, one finger problem

On a controller, accelerate, afterburner, drift, and ramjet want to be held at once. That is a binding complaint from the first weekend, and in-game rebinding is still thin. Put ramjet on a button you can reach without leaving accelerate. If you cannot, stop using ramjet in traffic. Afterburner will win more tours than a boost you fat-finger into a wall.
