If you bought Galactic Racer to queue a private four-stack, the October 6 build does not have that menu. Custom lobbies did not ship. Friends-only races did not ship. A 0.4-hour refund file is explicit: more than two other friends means you split the party, and even three of you still drop into a lobby with randoms.

Fuse Games already said the **group size cap moves from three to six** in a later update. That is a bigger party, not a private room. Until that note lands, treat online as public tour races. Split-screen is a separate no. See [split-screen status](/split-screen-multiplayer-status/) and [group size](/multiplayer-group-size/).

## What to play instead

- Run the campaign tour. That is the content the Very Positive pile is actually recommending.
- If you still want online, cap the Discord at three and accept the fill.
- Keybinds are a different gap. The store lists full controller support. In-game rebinding is still the complaint. See [Steam features](/steam-features-controller/).

A sale does not add a private lobby. The doubling patch will not add one either unless the notes say so.
