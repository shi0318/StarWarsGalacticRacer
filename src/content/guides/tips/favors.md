Between events the paddock offers work. A favor is one of those jobs: a challenge, a delivery of reputation, a thing a rival wants done before the next light. It pays credits or a part. It does not open a hidden chapter.

## How to spend one

- Do it before you lock the next event if the reward is a part you need for that planet. After you lock, the event does not reopen.
- Skip it if it sends you into a planet you are not built for. A favor that ends the tour is a bad wage.
- Hibi still gets the credits. A favor is not a second shop.

If a trophy names favors, it wants the job completed, not the dialogue skipped. Patch 1.0.11 skips more scenes. It does not complete the job for you.
