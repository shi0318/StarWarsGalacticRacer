Quick race is strangers. A rivalry is a pilot the tour already knows. The campaign version is Kestar Bool. Smaller rivalries show up as challenges in the paddock before you ever see him.

## How it differs from a normal race

- The other pilot is the objective. A win on position that lets them finish clean can fail the rivalry condition.
- Shunts matter more, and so does resilience. A glass bike that wins Jakku loses a head-to-head the moment they choose the acid line.
- It is not a private lobby. You cannot invite a friend into a rivalry slot. That room did not ship.

The [Kestar page](/characters/kestar-bool/) is the last one. This page is the pattern: named pilot, extra condition, same physics.
