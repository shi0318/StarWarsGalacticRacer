The League Token is what a Backer supplies so you can take part in the tour. It is not free. You complete a contract to keep the Backer happy. A rivalry is an option that can put a token on the line. The October 20 note said get to know the pilot's style before you do that. One shunt is not a style. A full lap where you can point at the braking marker is a style.

On **October 26, 2026** the US page was **$59.99**, Very Positive, **5,713** English reviews. A strong label does not make the token optional. It means a lot of people enjoyed the league enough to recommend a full-price game. They still had to sign something.

## Order

Qualify. Read Vranki, Sidon Ithano, and Q-33N. Sign the contract whose maneuver you have already landed in practice. Run that contract on a track you know. Only then look at a rival. Kestar is the campaign antagonist. Biddy Blas is the cantankerous example, a Crocin you might be nursing a grudge with, not a required bet. If your menu shows Firebug, watch the corner. Do not stake the token because a thumbnail said the name.

Decline the rivalry if the token is the only one keeping the tour open. The note called the rivalry an option. An option can wait one race. A failed crowd jump cannot. If you are under Vranki, fly the jump in clean air. Leave the draft before the jump marker. Crait's salt can blind you in the wake. Leave earlier.

## What the price does not unlock

**$59.99** does not unlock a private lobby or split-screen. Cross-play is the online meeting. Game Pass and Switch 2 were not on the October 26 button. A rivalry search will not add them. Two copies and a public queue are the couch. Say that before someone buys a second pad for one machine.

5,713 English reviews are not a stat block for Biddy. Rank numbers in secondary guides are hints until your own multiplayer menu shows the unlock. The store page did not print "rank 26." Do not bet a token on a number you have not seen in your game.

## After you can name the brake

Then the stake is a decision, not a reflex. If you lose the token, go back to the Backer contract. Do not immediately pick a second rival to win it back. That is how a run ends. **49** achievements. The rare 0.1% pops from earlier global samples are not a rivalry checklist. A clean contract keeps the tour funded. The Very Positive page already recommended the game. It will not replace a token you threw at a pilot you had not watched.

Qualify, sign Vranki or Sidon or Q-33N only if you can land the maneuver, run that contract, then watch a rival for one full lap. October 26 was $59.99, Very Positive, 5,713 English reviews. Kestar is the campaign antagonist. Biddy is an example, not a required bet. Decline the token stake if it is the only token keeping the tour open. A jump contract is not a rivalry. Cross-play is not split-screen. Game Pass was not on the button. Rank numbers that are not in your menu are hints. 49 achievements will not replace a token you threw away. One brake point, then the stake, or no stake.
