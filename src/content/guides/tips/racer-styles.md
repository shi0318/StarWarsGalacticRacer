A racer style is a way of spending the tour. It is not a fifth vehicle class, and it is not a livery. Landspeeder, bike, skim speeder, and podracer still decide the physics. The style decides which events, bets, and favors you are fishing for.

## How to pick one

- If you are still learning lines, do not pick a style that demands clean golds. Take the one that forgives a wreck. Resilience still comes first. A style does not replace it.
- If you are in the pod stretch, the style has to survive a separate grid. A speeder style does not carry its habits onto Sebulba's event.
- If a trophy names a style, read the condition before you respec. Some pops want you to finish a tour inside that style, not merely to open the menu.

The [build order](/tips/campaign-builds/) is the parts. This page is the label on the tour. Paint is still last.
