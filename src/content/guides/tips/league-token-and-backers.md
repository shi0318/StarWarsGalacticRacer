![A pod at a stadium during STAR WARS: Galactic Racer](/images/guide/stadium-pod.webp)

Once you qualify for the **Galactic League** in the single-player campaign, you have to start choosing **Backers**. Fuse Games' own Rivals and Backers note, posted **October 20, 2026**, is plain about why. Backers supply the **League Token** you need to take part in the tour. They do not supply it for free. You complete contracts to keep the Backer happy, and those contracts are what keep the resources coming during the tour.

The named choices in that note are **Vranki**, **Sidon Ithano**, and **Q-33N**. Vranki the Hutt is the one tied, in the same post, to dazzling jumps that delight the crowd. That is a contract shape, not a hidden stat. If the contract is a jump, practice the jump before you promise the token. A token you lose because you ignored the contract is a token you do not have for the next race.

STAR WARS: Galactic Racer has been out since **October 6, 2026**. On October 20 the Steam price was still **$59.99** with no discount. Reviews were Very Positive: **7,372** positive out of **8,494**. The Backer system is part of why a second run is not the first run. The note says no two runs are the same once contracts and rivalries are in play.

## What the token is for

The League Token is the entry to the tour. It is not a cosmetic and it is not a credit pack you farm on the side. The post says you need it to take part. That means a session with no token is a session that is not in the tour, no matter how clean the qualifying lap was.

Qualifying is the gate before this choice. [How to qualify](/tips/how-to-qualify/) is the lap. This page starts after that lap is done. Do not reopen the Backer menu during a hot lap because a contract popped. Finish the race you are in. Sign the contract in the menu the note describes, when you are choosing who backs the tour.

Resources from the Backer are the other half. The note says a happy Backer provides the resources you need during the tour. It does not print a credit number. So the practical rule is: do the contract the Backer asked for, then look at what actually arrived. If the jumps were the contract and you skipped them, do not expect the resource line to keep paying. The post already said the token is not free.

## How to pick among the three

Vranki, Sidon Ithano, and Q-33N are the three names the October 20 note asks you to choose between. They are not a tier list. The note does not say one of them pays more. It says you choose, and then you work.

Pick Vranki if you are willing to fly the crowd contract. The example in the post is dazzling jumps. If your pod cannot hold a jump on the track you are about to enter, that contract is a fail waiting on a signature. Learn the jump on a clean lap first. [One clean lap](/tips/one-clean-lap/) is the drill. A contract is a bad place to learn a new input.

Pick Sidon Ithano or Q-33N when the contract they offer matches a lap you can already drive. The note does not spell their contracts out in the same sentence as Vranki's jumps. Read the in-game contract before you confirm. A famous name is not a stat block. The contract text is the stat block.

Do not change Backers because a rival is loud. Rivals are the other system in the same video. [Choosing a rival](/tips/choose-your-rival/) is a separate decision, and the note says you can put a League Token on the line in that rivalry. Spending the token to enter the tour and then betting the same kind of stake on a pilot you have not watched is two spends. Do the tour contract first.

## What a contract changes on track

A jump contract changes the line. You leave the draft earlier if the jump marker is the thing the Backer is scoring, not the overtake. [When to leave the draft](/tips/when-to-leave-the-draft/) still applies: the braking marker is an exit, and a jump marker is an earlier exit than that. Staying in the wake through a required jump is how you miss the contract and the apex in the same second.

A contract does not change the pod's class ability. If the hitch is the car, fix the hitch. The Backer cannot pay you out of a machine that will not take the jump. [Match the pod to the hazard](/tips/match-the-pod-to-the-hazard/) before you sign a crowd contract on a track the pod cannot survive.

Crait's salt flat is still the visibility exception. A jump you cannot see because you stayed in the draft too long is a failed contract, not a brave one. Leave the wake, take the jump in air you can see, then look for the next draft after the landing.

## A signing order

1. Qualify. No League conversation before that gate.
2. Read the three contracts. Note which one asks for a maneuver you can already do.
3. Sign that Backer. Ignore the name if the maneuver is the one you crash.
4. Run the contract on a track you know before you run it on a new planet.
5. Only then look at a rivalry. The token is the tour. The rivalry can put a token on the line. Do not do both blind.

Steam lists **49** achievements. On October 20 the common end of the global board had **Achievement10** at **92.3%** and **Achievement23** at **83.7%**. The rare end was almost empty: **Achievement48**, **Achievement47**, and **Achievement36** were each **0.1%**. A Backer contract will not pop a 0.1% achievement by itself. It will keep the tour funded if you fly what you signed.

The price is still **$59.99**. Minimum spec is Windows 10, an i5-8400 or Ryzen 5 2600, **12 GB** of RAM, an RTX 2060 or equivalent with 6 GB, and **50 GB** on an SSD. A contract does not lower that. If the jump stutters, the contract fails for a hardware reason, and no Hutt is going to patch your SSD.

Bill Lane and Nathaniel Daw are the two names on the video. The systems they describe are the systems to play: a token, a contract, three Backers, and a tour that changes when you ignore the signature. Paint the pod after the contract is something you can fly.
