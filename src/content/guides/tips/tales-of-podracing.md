Tales of Podracing is the pod half of the game. It is not a speeder skin. Pods are faster and larger, so they race alone. Sebulba sits on that side of the roster and will not wave Shade at Kestar Bool in a pod without the practice.

## Two doors

- **Arcade** opens pods immediately. Use it if the trophy or the itch is "drive a pod tonight."
- **Campaign** gates the story stretch. Hours of speeder tours do not unlock it early. That matches what players reported in the first weekend.

Do not gold a speeder track and expect the pod tale to tick. The grids do not share events. The [Sebulba page](/characters/sebulba/) is the character. The [podracer page](/vehicles/podracers/) is the driving. This page is which door you are in.
