Three speeder classes share the same grid, and the podracer runs its own. The class is the physics — a racer style does not change it, and a Deluxe hull does not change it either. What separates the three speeders is one signature trait each: **Drift**, **Kinetic Burst** and **Knife Edge**. Each one wins on a different kind of corner.

## The three speeder classes

| Class | Ability | Wins on | Loses on |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Landspeeder** | **Drift** | Hazard tracks, contact-heavy packs, learning a new planet | Flowing circuits where the bike is simply quicker |
| **Speeder bike** | **Kinetic Burst** | Stop-start layouts with real braking zones | Anything where one shunt ends the race |
| **Skim speeder** | **Knife Edge** | Long linked corners where you can hold momentum | Any corner where you scrub speed |

**Landspeeders** are the class the launch notes call the tanks of the track: strong acceleration and top speed, enough resilience to take a hit, and **Drift**, the easiest-to-control corner trait of the three. If you cannot yet finish a lap without touching a wall, this is the class, and it stays the right class on the hazard planets long after you stop being a beginner.

**Kinetic Burst** is a kinetic blast that pushes the bike off a wall and gives a surge out of a well-timed drift exit. That makes the bike fastest exactly where most players are slowest — it rewards braking late and firing out of the turn instead of coasting in. The bike also has the best acceleration, so it recovers fastest after contact. The cost is that it is a glass cannon: a bad shunt ends a bike's lap faster than it ends a landspeeder's.

**Knife Edge** rolls the skim speeder onto one edge and drives that side into the ground to force it round a corner, and it can smash a rival who is alongside. The skim speeder has the **highest top speed** of the three and the biggest skill gap: it only pays off when you hold momentum through every corner. Learn the line and it keeps speed through a sequence that costs the other classes two separate corner exits.

## Podracers are not a fourth option

Pods race on their own grid because of speed and size. Breakneck pace, a much larger footprint, and almost no room to fix a mistake — a contact line that works in a landspeeder is a wall in a pod. Arcade pod events are available from the menu immediately; campaign pods open as the tour reaches that stretch.

Do not main a pod for the speeder tour. Treat it as a second game you also own. See the [podracer page](/vehicles/podracers/).

## Which to bring

1. **Learning a planet:** landspeeder, every time. A tour ends when you crash out too many times, not when you finish fourth.
2. **A circuit you already know:** bike if it has braking zones, skim speeder if it has linked corners.
3. **A contact-heavy multiplayer tour:** landspeeder, unless you are confident you can stay out of the pack.

Resilience comes before speed in the [build order](/tips/campaign-builds/) for the same reason.
