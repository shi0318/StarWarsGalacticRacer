## Before you farm anything

Star Wars Galactic Racer launches **October 6, 2026**. There is no confirmed currency name, payout table, or farming route yet. This page explains **how credit economies usually work in racing games** so you're ready on day one — and it will be rewritten with real, tested numbers the moment the game is live.

📖 The game is built around **runs** in the Galactic League — a high-stakes, roguelike-flavoured loop. In games like that, a run's payout usually scales with how far you push before cashing out, which changes farming strategy (see below). The exact soft currency for unlocks and upgrades is unconfirmed.

## Where credits usually come from

Expect income from a mix of these sources:

- **Race placement** — higher finishes pay more. Winning is almost always the best rate.
- **Run / championship completion** — bonus lump sums for finishing a series or a full run.
- **First-time clears** — many racers pay a one-off bonus the first time you beat an event.
- **Challenges / objectives** — "win without being taken down," "hit top speed X," etc.

## The efficient-farming principle

The universal rule in racing games: **the best farm is the shortest event you can consistently win at the highest payout tier.** Once the game is out we'll identify that specific race. Until then, the method is:

1. Find the shortest race or run you can win reliably
2. Check its payout vs its length (credits per minute, not credits per race)
3. Repeat that event, not the longest or flashiest one

A two-minute race you win every time beats a five-minute race you win half the time.

📖 If runs really are roguelike, watch for a **risk/reward cash-out**: pushing one more race for a bigger pot but risking a wipe that loses it. The efficient play is usually to bank a guaranteed payout you can repeat, not to gamble a whole run.

## Don't neglect first-time bonuses

Early on, the fastest way to build a bankroll is usually **clearing new events once each** to sweep up first-time bonuses, rather than grinding one race. Spread wide first, then pick a repeatable farm once the bonuses dry up.
