Credits come from finishing events, then leave through Hibi's workshop. There is no separate farming mode, and there is no code that fills the wallet. A tour that wrecks out is the expensive way to learn a planet.

## What is worth buying

- Resilience first. It is the purchase that lets the next event pay you.
- Afterburner second.
- Ramjet third, and only if you already lift.
- Livery never, until the above is done.

Arcade is the cleaner loop if you want repetitions of one planet without betting the whole tour. It pays practice more than it pays a secret multiplier. Do not expect a delivery quest or a bar mini-game to be the economy. Players have asked for exactly that. It is not in the launch build.

The spending order is the [build guide](/tips/campaign-builds/). The person who takes the credits is [Hibi](/characters/hibi/).
