Pods and speeders do not share a button. A speeder quick race is the queue players mean when they say they showed up and left. Pod events sit apart from it. A plain pod quick-race button is something players are still asking for. Do not assume the speeder lobby includes Sebulba's grid because the planet name matches.

On **October 25, 2026** the US Steam page listed the game at **$59.99**, Very Positive, **5,713** English reviews. This check did not print a 30-day split, so none is quoted. 5,713 Very Positive reviews do not merge the queues. They mean a lot of people liked the race they actually entered.

## How to count a night

If you are answering "how many tracks," split the count by vehicle. One planet can be a speeder lap and a pod event. Counting both as one circuit hides the queue you have not unlocked. Counting both as two planets inflates the disc. Label the note: speeder, pod, or tour.

The six-race tour is a mode. Upgrades between races, then a showdown, is not six new planets. Do not add the tour on top of every planet and call it the track total. The store page never printed a single integer. Your menu is the census. This page will not invent one to win a search.

## What the queue does not change

Cross-play is on across PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. It does not put a pod and a speeder in the same lobby. Split-screen did not ship. Private lobbies did not ship. A friend on another platform can meet you in the queue you both selected. They cannot meet you in a queue you only assumed existed.

Game Pass and Switch 2 were not on the launch list, and the October 25 button did not add them. A pod search will not reveal a hidden Nintendo build. **$59.99** is the Windows price until the button changes.

Biddy Blas and Kestar are pilots, not queues. Learn a braking point before you stake a League Token. The token is a Backer contract, not a pod unlock. Vranki's jump is a contract on the vehicle you signed it for. Do not sign a jump on a pod you have not landed.

## One queue tonight

Enter the queue the menu names. Write down whether it was speeder or pod. Leave the draft at the braking marker in that race, not in a race you are not in. Minimum spec is an SSD, **50 GB**, **12 GB** of RAM. A hitch at the marker is a load until the install moves. Then it is a rival.

**49** achievements. None of the numbers on the October 25 page say "win every queue." Open the in-game list if you care. 5,713 Very Positive English reviews are the crowd in the races they could find. Find the button that matches the vehicle in your hands. The other button can wait.
