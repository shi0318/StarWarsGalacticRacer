The draft is free speed while the car ahead is on the straight. It is a crash if you are still tucked in when the braking marker starts. Pull out, take your own line, then look for the next wake after the corner.

Crait's salt flat is the special case: staying in the draft can blind you. If the screen washes out, leave the wake even if the straight is not over. The longer note is [draft and slipstream](/tips/draft-and-slipstream/). This page is only the exit.

## Exit rules

1. See the braking marker. Leave.
2. See a hazard the planet page names. Leave before it, not through it.
3. A shunt from inside the draft is a contact you did not set up. [Set up the shunt](/tips/set-up-the-shunt/) on the straight, in clean air.

Do not change pods because a draft felt slow. That feeling is usually a hitch. [Pod versus hazard](/tips/match-the-pod-to-the-hazard/).
