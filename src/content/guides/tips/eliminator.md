Eliminator is a shrinking field. Places disappear as the race continues. It is not the six-race online tour, and it is not a single quick race where fourth still pays.

## How to survive one

- Do not ramjet the opening straight if the cut is behind you. A burned speeder is an elimination, not a bad sector.
- Hazards count. Acid on Sentinel One and heat on Lantaana remove you without a rival having to shunt. Resilience is the buy.
- Position is the objective once the field is small. A style or a side bet that wants a risk you cannot afford should wait for a normal race.

If a trophy names Eliminator, finish the event type. Queueing quick race six times will not pop it. The [multiplayer page](/tips/multiplayer-tour/) is the other online shape, so you can tell them apart.
