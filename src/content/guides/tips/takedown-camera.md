A first-week file names the guaranteed wall: the takedown camera faces **behind** your speeder while the track is still in front. Crash playback already runs long. This is worse, because the animation is also steering you. It is not a driver-floor boot fail. It is not a DLSS option. Those are the [driver hotfix](/driver-hotfix/).

Do not mash ramjet during the cam. Let the playback finish, then correct. If the game actually hard-crashes mid-race, that is a different pile — several files report race crashes on settings that should not stress the box. Update drivers, then file the race crash separately from the kill cam.

## How to keep the speeder

- Take the takedown on a straight if you can. A canyon wall during the reverse cam is a wreck-out.
- Crash animations that “take way too long” are the same family. They are not a private-lobby missing menu.
- Rubber-band AI in campaign is a third complaint. See [rubberbanding](/rubberbanding-ai/). Online bumper-cars are a fourth. Do not mix them.

The campaign tour still rewards the shunt. This page is the camera that turns a legal hit into a wall.
