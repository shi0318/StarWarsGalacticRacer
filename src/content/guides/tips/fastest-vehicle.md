## The honest answer, pre-launch

Nobody has confirmed the fastest vehicle in Star Wars Galactic Racer — the game releases **2026-10-06** and no stat tables are public. What we *do* know is official: the game features **four classes of repulsorcraft**, each with "distinct physics and playstyles." This page ranks those classes by **expected** raw top speed (📖) and updates to a tested, ranked list once the game is out.

## The four confirmed classes

✅ Officially named on the Steam page:

- **Podracers** — twin-engine racing pods
- **Speeder bikes** — agile personal craft
- **Skim speeders** — low-slung ground-huggers
- **Landspeeders** — balanced repulsorcraft

See the [vehicles list](/vehicles/) for the full breakdown and confirmation status on each class.

## Projected top-speed tiers (lore-based)

This ranks the classes by **expected** raw top speed, not by how easy they are to drive. It is inference, not confirmation.

### S — likely highest ceiling
- **Podracers** — built around raw straight-line speed. Highest ceiling, hardest to control.

### A — fast and more forgiving
- **Speeder bikes** — quick with better agility than a pod; suited to tight, technical circuits.
- **Skim speeders** — low and fast, likely trading some cornering for pace.

### B — balanced / beginner
- **Landspeeders** — likely tuned as an approachable, balanced entry class rather than a top-speed monster.

## Fastest vehicle ≠ best vehicle

The highest top speed almost never wins races on its own. A pod that tops out fastest but slides into every wall loses to a mid-speed vehicle you can actually hold on the racing line. On technical circuits, **acceleration and handling** frequently beat raw speed.

That's why our [best build](/tips/best-build/) guide leads with handling for new players — a vehicle you can drive flat-out beats a faster one you can't.
