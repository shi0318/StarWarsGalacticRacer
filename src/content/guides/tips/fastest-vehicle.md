Podracers are the fastest thing in the game. They reach breakneck speed and leave no room for error, which is why they race on their own grid. A tier list that puts a pod above a landspeeder is describing two events.

## On the speeder grid

Speeder bikes are the fast fragile option. Kinetic Burst is an exit, not a top-speed stat. Skim speeders are the flowing middle. Landspeeders are the tanks, and they are the ones that finish Sentinel One and Lantaana.

The Deluxe hulls do not change this. Kor Sarun: Ciza T is a bike. It is not a secret faster class. Buying Deluxe does not buy a multiplayer advantage.

Drive the pod in Arcade or in the pod queue. Drive the landspeeder when the tour matters. Class notes are on the [vehicle list](/vehicles/).
