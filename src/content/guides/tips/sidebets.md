A side bet is an extra condition on a race you were already going to run. Hit it, the purse pays. Miss it, you still have the race result and you are poorer than the pilot who did not take the bet.

## When to take one

- Take it on a planet you have already seen. Jakku on tour one is a bad bet. Jakku on tour three is a purse.
- Do not take a bet that requires a ramjet hold if you still explode the speeder. The [build page](/tips/campaign-builds/) is the prerequisite.
- Credits from a bet go to the same Hibi shop as race credits. There is no separate wallet.

A trophy that names side bets wants the condition, not a menu visit. If you cannot see the stake before the lights, you are not in a bet. You are in a normal race.
