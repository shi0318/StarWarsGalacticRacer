**Biddy Blas** is the name the October 20, 2026 Rivals and Backers note uses when it wants a cantankerous rivalry. The line is that you might be nursing a growing grudge with the Crocin. It is an example of the system, not a hidden best vehicle and not a required target. If your decisions escalated Biddy, that is the pilot in front of you. If they did not, Kestar is still the campaign antagonist, and Biddy can wait.

STAR WARS: Galactic Racer launched **October 6, 2026**. On October 20 Steam was Very Positive, **7,372** positive out of **8,494**, at **$59.99** with no discount. Searches for Biddy spiked with the video. A search spike is not a stat block. The note does not print Biddy's braking point, top speed, or a damage number.

## What is safe to say

Biddy Blas is a Crocin. The same post places that racer under the Bool banner in the wider roster talk, and calls the rivalry cantankerous. Multiplayer listings used by this site put Biddy at a later rank, with a Firebug style, and they disagree with each other enough that a rank number should not be treated as a store-page fact. The store page did not print "unlock at rank 26." A guide that did is a guide. Use it as a hint. Confirm the unlock in your own multiplayer menu.

Firebug, if that is the style your build shows, is a reason to watch the corner, not a reason to bet a **League Token** on the first meeting. The October 20 note says get to know a rival's racing style before the token is on the line. One shunt is not a style. A full lap where you can say where they brake is a style.

Kestar remains the campaign antagonist. Biddy is the example of "other pilots can escalate." Do not skip Kestar's qualify because a thumbnail said Biddy is the real boss. [How to qualify](/tips/how-to-qualify/) is still the gate. The rivalry menu is after it.

## How to race the grudge if it is yours

Leave the draft at the braking marker. Leave earlier if Biddy punts in the corner and you have seen that punt once. [Set up the shunt](/tips/set-up-the-shunt/) on the straight, in clean air. An aerial punt is a crash with a famous witness. Crait's salt flat can blind you if you stay in the wake. A rival who wants you blind will sit there. Leave anyway.

Do not change pods because one shove felt slow. Match the pod to the planet hazard, then go back to the pilot. A new car plus a new grudge is two lessons. You will learn neither.

Vranki's jump contract is a separate signature. If you are under that contract, fly the jump you signed. Decline the token stake until the landing is clean. The note calls the rivalry an option. An option can wait one race. A failed crowd jump cannot.

## What Biddy does not change

Cross-play is on across PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. Split-screen is not. Private lobbies are not. Biddy is not a local-coop unlock. Two copies and an online queue are the couch solution, and even that is a public queue.

Switch 2 and Game Pass were not on the launch list. A Biddy search will not reveal a hidden Nintendo build. The platforms page is the short no. This page is the pilot.

**49** achievements. **Achievement10** was **92.3%** on October 20. Three of the rarest in the global sample were **0.1%**. Biddy is not those pops unless the in-game list says so. A rivalry is a lap with a name on it. Learn the brake. Keep the token for the tour until you can say where the Crocin punts. Then decide if the grudge is worth the stake.


## One more pass before you close the menu

The numbers on this page were checked against the October 20, 2026 Steam listing, not against a launch-week memory. Price, review count, achievement total, and the patch lines named above are the facts to plan a session around. A thumbnail that contradicts them is a thumbnail. Play the build you have. Write down the result in the game's own words. Then decide whether the next door, the next bind, or the next purchase is the one this page was about.

If the machine is under the minimum spec printed on the store page, fix that before you fix the tactic. An SSD requirement is a disk requirement. A RAM number is a RAM number. A driver popup is a driver popup. None of those become optional because a guide got longer. The tactic still matters after the machine holds a frame. It does not matter during a hitch you mistook for an enemy tell.

Stop when the one test in the list above has a yes or a no. A second system, a second cosmetic, and a second difficulty change in the same minute will make the yes useless. Come back for the second change after the first one is written down. The rest of the site can wait until that note exists.

Watch one full lap before the token moves. Biddy Blas can be the Crocin in your rivalry and still not be the pilot you bet on tonight. Kestar is the campaign antagonist. The League Token is the tour entry a Backer supplies. A Firebug style, if your menu shows it, is a corner to observe, not a number to trust from a second-hand rank list. Cross-play does not make the grudge local. Split-screen did not ship. Learn the brake, keep the token, and let the cantankerous example stay an example until you can say where the punt happens.

One lap, one brake point, one decision about the token. That is a complete use of the name. The rest of the roster can wait for a later rank.


Kestar will still be in the campaign when you get back. The Crocin will still be cantankerous. The token should still be in the tour until you can point at the corner where the punt happens.
