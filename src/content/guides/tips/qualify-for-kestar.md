Kestar Bool is not a garage unlock and not a quick-race boss. He is the champion the campaign tour is climbing toward. Darius Pax recruits Shade to take that title. You qualify by still having a tour when the late events arrive.

## What has to be true

- You are in the campaign, not only in Arcade. Arcade can teach a planet. It does not walk you into his race.
- The tour has not wrecked out. Too many crashes end it. A fresh tour is a new qualification, with only some perks carried.
- Resilience is on the speeder before Sentinel One and Lantaana. A glass bike that was fine on Jakku does not arrive.
- Derven Acos is the arena the late tour points at. Treat the planets before it as checks, not as optional color.

The [Kestar page](/characters/kestar-bool/) is who he is. The [run guide](/campaign-run-planning/) is the structure. This page is the mistake: people hunt a "qualify" button. There isn't one. There is a tour that is still alive.
