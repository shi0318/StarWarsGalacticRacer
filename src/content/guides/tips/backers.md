A backer is someone paying for your tour because they want a result. Darius Pax is the story version: he funds Shade because he wants the League back. Smaller backers want a placement, a bet, or a favor.

## What they are not

- Not the Deluxe upgrade. That is a store purchase. A backer is inside the tour.
- Not a permanent stat. If the tour wrecks out, read what carried. Assume the money did not.
- Not a reason to skip resilience. A backer who wants a win on Sentinel One is asking you to survive acid, not to look sponsored.

Take the backer whose condition matches the planet you already drive well. Refuse the one that requires a gold you have never set. The [credits page](/tips/credits-farming/) is where the purse goes after.
