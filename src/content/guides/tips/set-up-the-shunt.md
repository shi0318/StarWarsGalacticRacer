A **shunt** or **takedown** that starts in the braking zone is a crash you caused. Start the contact on the straight, finish it before the apex, and leave the corner for the racing line.

The launch combat terms are shunt and takedown. They are not a grab you can hold through a hairpin. If the camera hits the wall during the animation, that is the [takedown camera](/tips/takedown-camera/) problem. Fix the view after the contact, do not add a second shunt to compensate.

## A clean contact

1. Pick the rival on the straight, not in the braking marker.
2. Finish the shunt so your nose is still pointed at the apex.
3. If the camera sticks, reset it before the next contact. A stuck camera plus a second shunt is how a lead becomes a wall.

Track hazards still end runs faster than a missed shunt. Open the planet page for the hazard, then come back here for the contact.
