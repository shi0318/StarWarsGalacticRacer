Landspeeders, podracers, skim speeders, and speeder bikes are different answers to different ground. Open the [vehicle hub](/vehicles/) and the planet page together. Do not swap pods because a lap hitch felt slow. That hitch has its own checks.

Derven Acos is the finale, not a vehicle class. A bike that survived a snow gate is not automatically the finale pick.

## A short match

- Read the hazard first. [Tracks](/tracks/).
- If the failure is a wall during a takedown, fix the camera and the shunt timing. The pod did not cause that.
- If the failure is a hitch every few races, stop at [DLSS and the driver hotfix](/tips/dlss-45-settings/) before the garage.

A Golden Joystick nomination does not add a faster class. It does not change this order.
