There are two boosts and they are not the same tool. The **afterburner** is a short boost that burns the heat your machine builds up, then recharges during the race. The **ramjet** is the turbo — on a pad you click the right stick to switch to it. It is much stronger and helps you knock rivals off the line, but it overheats if you keep running it — and an overheated ramjet destroys the speeder. That is not a damage tick. That is the run.

## When to use which

| Situation | Boost | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Corner exit | Afterburner | Short burst; no overheat risk while steering |
| Long clean straight | Ramjet | The only place the hold is free |
| Closing a gap before a shunt | Afterburner | You need the hands for the contact line |
| Lava caves on Lantaana | Neither, mostly | Heat builds while you are already in a heat zone |
| Ando Prime heat tunnels | Afterburner | You are threading a line, not racing a straight |
| A line you have not learned yet | Afterburner | A ramjet wreck on an unknown corner costs the tour |

## The overheat rule

Lift before the gauge finishes, not when the gauge finishes. The failure case is always the same: a player holds ramjet into a cave or a heat section because the straight *looked* long enough, and the speeder explodes in a place where there is no line to recover onto.

On Lantaana, lava caves are the whole circuit and heat buildup is the published hazard. On Ando Prime the heat tunnels are something you have to hit, which means you are already carrying heat you did not choose. Ramjet in either place is a decision to gamble the tour on a corner.

## The controller problem nobody solves for you

Accelerate, afterburner, drift and ramjet all want to be held at the same time, and in-game rebinding is thin. Full controller support is confirmed on the store listing; a comfortable layout is not.

Put ramjet on a button you can press without releasing accelerate. If no such button exists on your pad, **stop using ramjet in traffic**. An afterburner you can reach wins more tours than a ramjet you fat-finger into a wall.

## Buy them in this order

Resilience, then afterburner, then ramjet. Buying ramjet first and learning the line with it is the most expensive mistake in the shop, because the part that kills you is the part you bought. The full order is on the [campaign build page](/tips/campaign-builds/).
