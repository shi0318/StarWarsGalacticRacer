The six-race tour is a mode: upgrades between races, then a showdown. It is the campaign loop with strangers shunting you. It is not six new planets, and it is not the speeder quick race. Quick race is show up, race, leave. The tour is the longer queue. Count it once, as a mode, or you will double-count every planet you already named.

On **October 26, 2026** the US page was **$59.99**, Very Positive, **5,713** English reviews, no 30-day split in this check. People are playing both queues. The label does not tell you which button you pressed.

## How to enter it on purpose

Qualify for the league before you treat a tour as your evening. The Backer menu is after that gate. Vranki, Sidon Ithano, and Q-33N are contracts, not tour settings. Sign the contract you can fly, then queue the tour if you want upgrades between races. Do not sign a jump contract and discover the tour on the same lap. One new system.

A rival stake is the other system. The October 20 note said learn the pilot's line before a League Token is on it. The tour will put strangers in your draft whether or not you have a named grudge. Leave the wake at the braking marker anyway. Crait can blind you if you stay tucked in. A tour upgrade will not see through salt.

Pods stay in their own queue. Do not enter a speeder tour and expect Sebulba. If the showdown is a pod event, the menu will say so. Read the button.

## What a Very Positive tour still crashes

Staying in the draft through a required jump. Changing pods because one shunt felt slow. Betting the token on a pilot you have not watched for a full lap. Those are player errors the 5,713 reviews did not delete. **$59.99** does not include a spotter.

Cross-play means the stranger can be on PS5 or Xbox. It does not mean split-screen, and it does not mean a private lobby. Two copies and a public tour are the couch version. Say that plainly if someone asks to play on one machine.

Switch 2 and Game Pass were not on this button. A six-race search will not create them. Minimum spec is an SSD and **12 GB** of RAM. A hitch between races is a load. Move the install before you blame the upgrade screen.

## After one tour

Write down whether you finished, which contract you were under, and where you got punted. That note is more useful than a track total. The store page never printed one. The tour is a mode on top of circuits you can already name. Race it once on a contract you can land. Then decide if the quick race was the queue you actually wanted.
