The US Steam page on **October 25, 2026** listed STAR WARS: Galactic Racer at **$59.99**. No discount was shown. English reviews were Very Positive, **5,625** for the game. That check did not show a last-30-days split, so this page does not invent one. Full price and a strong English label is the offer. Game Pass is not part of it.

The game launched **October 6** on PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. Owning it on Xbox is not the same as a subscription listing. Switch 2 was not in the launch note. Nineteen days later, the Steam button had not become either of those rumors.

## What $59.99 buys

The Windows build: campaign, league, Backers, rivals, online cross-play, pods and speeders in separate queues. Not a second local player. Not a private lobby. Not a hidden Nintendo layout. Not a printed track total. You count circuits you can enter. You do not pay extra for a number the page never printed.

Cross-play means a friend on PS5 or Xbox can meet you if they also own it. It does not mean their copy is free because yours was **$59.99**. Two purchases, one public queue. That is the couch workaround. It is not split-screen.

## When full price is the right call

Buy it if you want the league and 5,625 Very Positive English reviews are enough company. The October 25 page is not asking you to wait for a sale it does not show.

Wait if the only reason you are here is Game Pass or Switch 2. Those require a different store page. Waiting on Steam to grow a Nintendo logo is how a month disappears.

If you own it, stop reading the price. Qualify. Sign a contract you can fly. Leave the draft before the braking marker. A **$59.99** race still crashes if you stay in the wake through a jump you promised Vranki.
