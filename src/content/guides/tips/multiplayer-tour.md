There are two online shapes, and they are not the same queue.

**Quick race** is the one players currently prefer. You show up, you race, you leave. **The tour** is six races with upgrades between them, other pilots shunting you on purpose, and a showdown on Derven Acos. It is the campaign's run structure dropped into matchmaking. Gauntlet play sits beside those two and, so far, draws more complaints than the quick race.

## Before you ready up

- Resilience before the acid planet. The tour will get there. A glass bike does not.
- Pods are not in the speeder quick race. Pod-focused events exist, and players are still asking for a plain pod quick race. Do not queue the wrong one and then call it empty.
- Cross-play is on. You are not in a PC-only lobby.

## What is actually missing

First-weekend reviews keep asking for the same three things: in-game control rebinding, a field-of-view slider for the walking paddock, and private lobbies. None of those are in the game as shipped. There is also no custom race for you and a friend. Plan around that. Do not wait in a menu for a private room that is not there.

If the tour feels short, it is. The complaint about track count is common, and it is fair. Learn the planets that exist — the hazard changes between them more than the corner count does — instead of expecting a 20-course cup.
