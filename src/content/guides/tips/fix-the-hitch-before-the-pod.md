A hitch every few races is not the pod being slow, and it is not a failed boot check. Those are three different problems. Change one.

Install the driver hotfix if the hitch started after a GPU driver change. Then look at DLSS 4.5, the SSD baseline, and whether the stutter is a camera hit during a takedown. A landspeeder swap will not fix a driver hitch.

## Order of checks

1. [Race stutters](/tips/race-stutters/) — the hitch that is not a boot failure.
2. [Driver hotfix](/driver-hotfix/) — if the stutter arrived with a driver.
3. [DLSS 4.5](/tips/dlss-45-settings/) — published settings, not an invented FPS number.
4. Only then change the pod. Vehicle pages are for the hazard, not for a stutter.

HDR black screens and corrupted saves are a fourth bucket. Do not stack a pod change on top of a black screen. See [HDR](/hdr-black-screens/).
