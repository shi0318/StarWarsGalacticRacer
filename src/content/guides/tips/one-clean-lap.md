![A volcanic circuit in STAR WARS: Galactic Racer](/images/swgr-volcano-circuit.webp)

A planet page names the hazard that ends the run. Ice, a canyon wall, a dune jump. That hazard is lap one. A **shunt** is lap two. Adding contact before you can finish the lap clean is how a Very Positive racer becomes a wall.

The campaign does not number these into a cup. Pick the planet you can finish. [All settings](/tracks/all-tracks-planets/).

## Two laps

1. Lap one: no contact. Note the braking marker and the hazard. If the lap hitches, that is [the stutter check](/tips/fix-the-hitch-before-the-pod/), not a slow pod.
2. Lap two: set the shunt up on the straight and finish it before the apex. [Set up the shunt](/tips/set-up-the-shunt/).
3. If the camera sticks in the animation, reset it before the next rival. [Takedown camera](/tips/takedown-camera/).

Gold times stay on the event screen. Do not chase a copied time on lap one.
