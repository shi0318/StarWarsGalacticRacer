**STAR WARS: Galactic Racer** launched **October 6, 2026** on PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. Steam sells the Windows build for **$59.99**. Xbox Series X|S can own the game. Owning it on Xbox is not the same as it being on Game Pass. No launch-day Game Pass release was part of the store listing checked with the launch. A search for "game pass" does not create a subscription SKU.

On **October 20** reviews were Very Positive, **7,372** of **8,494**, and the Steam discount was still **0%**. People are playing it at full price. They are not playing a hidden Game Pass build that the Xbox store forgot to mention.

## What shipped

Three platforms. Cross-play across those three. Online PvP. A single-player campaign with Backers and rivals after you qualify. Pods and speeders in separate queues. That is the list in the launch note and on the Steam feature flags.

What did not ship: Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, local split-screen, and private lobbies. A Switch 2 search spike in the same week as the Game Pass search is the same kind of rumor. Nothing in the launch note names Nintendo. If a port or a Game Pass drop is announced later, it will be a dated store page. It will not be a menu icon inside the PS5 build you already own.

Check the Xbox store on the day you care if you are hoping for a subscription add. Do not treat a forum date as a calendar. The **$59.99** Steam price is the Windows price. Console prices can differ. Neither price becomes zero because a suggestion box offered "game pass" as a completion.

## What to do instead of waiting

Qualify. Pick a Backer whose contract you can fly. Vranki, Sidon Ithano, and Q-33N are the names on the October 20 note. The League Token is the tour entry, and it is not free. A Game Pass wait does not practice the jump. A owned copy does.

If the question is the couch, split-screen is not in the launch build. Two copies and an online quick race are the workaround, and the queue is public. Cross-play means the friend can be on a different one of the three platforms. It does not mean a second local player.

If the question is the machine, minimum spec is Windows 10, an i5-8400 or Ryzen 5 2600, **12 GB** of RAM, an RTX 2060-class GPU with 6 GB, and **50 GB** on an SSD. Recommended is **16 GB** and a 3060 Ti-class card. Game Pass would not have lowered that bar. An HDD will still hitch at the braking marker. Fix the disk before you blame a rival for a psychic punt.

No Switch 2 at launch. No Game Pass at launch. Yes on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S. Yes on cross-play. No on split-screen. No on private lobbies.
