The pre-launch "best build" was a guess. The post-launch one is a spending order.

<figure>
  <img src="/images/guide/livery-editor.webp" alt="Spend credits on parts, not on the paint in this bay" width="1400" height="788" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

1. Resilience, before Sentinel One and before a six-race online tour.
2. Afterburner, for traffic and for corners.
3. Ramjet, only after you can lift. Holding it through a lava cave ends the run.
4. Paint, banners, and the N-1 livery after the tour survives.

Class is part of the build. A landspeeder is the default. A bike wins a clean Crait exit and loses an acid pool. A pod is not in this ranking, because it is a different race.

The longer version, with a planet table, is the [campaign build guide](/tips/campaign-builds/). This page stays so the old URL still answers the search.
