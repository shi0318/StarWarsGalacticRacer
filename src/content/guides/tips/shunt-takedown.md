The launch description calls out three pieces of combat racing: **slam, shunt, and takedown**. They are the reason a clean lap is not the whole race. A shunt is the contact that moves a rival; a takedown is the finishing event that confirms the attack. Treat the two as a setup and a payoff instead of holding the attack button and hoping for a knockout.

## Build the approach

- Keep some **resilience** before looking for contact. The [campaign build order](/tips/campaign-builds/) puts resilience ahead of raw speed because a failed approach can wreck a tour, not just lose one corner.
- Enter from the outside of the turn and leave yourself a recovery line. A rival pushed into a wall is only useful if your own craft still has a road out.
- Use **afterburner** to close the gap and **ramjet** once the line is learned. Holding ramjet through a cave or heat section is a self-inflicted wreck; the [best-build page](/tips/best-build/) explains the spending order.

## Read the result before repeating the hit

The game does not publish a universal damage number for every vehicle class. Watch the race state instead:

| What you see | What it means | Next decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rival changes line but keeps racing | A shunt created space, not a takedown | Take the inside and protect the exit |
| Rival enters a takedown sequence | The attack has paid off | Hold the steering input for the recovery |
| Your craft loses resilience or leaves the line | The trade went badly | Stop forcing contact and rebuild the line |

The takedown camera can point backward and put the player into a wall. That is a known presentation problem, not a reason to chain another hit; use the [takedown-camera fix](/tips/takedown-camera/) when the replay leaves you facing the barrier.

## Pick the right place to fight

- **Quick Race:** a shunt is cheap information because the next race starts fresh. Learn how much room each class needs before taking the same contact into a tour.
- **Six-race multiplayer tour:** keep resilience and credits for the next event. The tour carries upgrades forward and ends at [Derven Acos](/tracks/derven-acos/), so a spectacular early takedown is not worth a wreck-out.
- **Pod events:** pods use their own grid. Do not copy a landspeeder contact line into a podracer cave; the [podracer guide](/vehicles/podracers/) treats that class separately.

## Controller and camera checklist

1. Put boost and the combat input where you can reach them without releasing steering. The Steam page confirms full controller support, but it does not promise a comfortable in-game rebinding layout; the [controller guide](/steam-features-controller/) records that gap.
2. Keep the camera wide enough to see the exit before starting contact.
3. If a takedown animation ends with the craft pointed at a wall, correct the line first. Firing again does not repair the camera.
4. In a tour, spend the next Hibi visit on resilience or the control part that fixes your repeated mistake. Cosmetics are last.

The useful rule is simple: shunt when you have an exit, take down when the rival is already committed, and leave the next corner alive.
